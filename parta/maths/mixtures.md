# PART A – MIXTURE

## 1. Mixture kya hota hai?

- Hindi: **Mishran**
- Jab **do ya do se zyada alag cheezein** mil jaati hain to mixture banta hai.
- Pani me pani milao to mixture nahi; pani me **doodh/alcohol** milao to mixture.

**Ex:** 50 L milk + 30 L water = **80 L mixture**

---

## 2. Ratio ke Basic Rules (yaad rakho)

### Rule 1: Ratio nikalna
- Milk 50 L, water 32 L → ratio = 50 : 32 = **25 : 16**
- Milk 40 L, water 124 L → 40 : 124 = **10 : 31**

### Rule 2: Quantity add karne par ratio
- Milk 50, water 45 → pani me 20 L add → water = 65 → ratio **50 : 65 = 10 : 13**

### Rule 3: Ek quantity pata ho
- Milk : water = 5 : 7, milk = 20 L → 5 units = 20 → 1 unit = 4 → **water = 7 × 4 = 28 L**

### Rule 4: Mixture ka total (ratio ka sum)
- Milk : water = 7 : 5, water = 15 L
- 5 units = 15 → 1 unit = 3
- **Mixture = (7 + 5) × 3 = 36 L** ("mixture" puchha hai to sum!)

### Rule 5: Total mixture diya ho
- Milk : water = 5 : 3, total = 40 L → 8 units = 40 → 1 unit = 5
- Milk = **25 L**, Water = **15 L**

### Rule 6: Difference diya ho
- Milk : water = 10 : 7, milk − water = 15 L
- 3 units = 15 → 1 unit = 5
- Milk = **50 L**, Water = **35 L**

**Master line:** Jaise hi **1 unit** ki value mil jaye, saari values mil jaati hain.

---

## 3. Pani Add Karne ka Rule ⭐

**Rule:** Agar **pani add** ho raha hai to **milk ka ratio SAME** rehna chahiye. Pani ka ratio badlega.
Milk ke units ko **barabar** karo (cross multiply), phir pani ke units ka difference nikalo.

### Ex1: Milk : water = 2 : 3. 10 L water add karne par 2 : 5 ho gaya.
- Milk same (2 = 2 ✔)
- Water 3 → 5 = **2 units** badhe = 10 L → **1 unit = 5**
- **Old:** Milk 2 × 5 = 10, Water 3 × 5 = 15
- **New:** Milk 10, Water 5 × 5 = 25

### Ex2: Milk : water = 8 : 3. 21 L water add karne par 4 : 5 ho gaya.
- Milk same karo: 4 → 8 (×2): new = 8 : 10
- Water 3 → 10 = **7 units** = 21 → 1 unit = 3
- **Old:** Milk 24, Water 9
- **New:** Milk 24, Water 30

### Ex3: Milk : water = 6 : 5. 66 L water add → 5 : 6 ho gaya.
- Milk same: (6 : 5) × 5 = 30 : 25; (5 : 6) × 6 = 30 : 36
- Water 25 → 36 = **11 units** = 66 → 1 unit = 6
- **Old:** Milk 180, Water 150
- **New:** Milk 180, Water 216

**Note:** Ye "1 unit" sirf **us hisse ke** ratios me use hota hai (old/new alag cases ke beech "dewar/gate" hai, par ek hi unit puri problem me liya ja sakta hai jab same system ho).

---

## 4. Mixture Nikalne ka Rule ⭐⭐

**Rule:** Jab hum mixture me se kuch **nikalte hain**, to **ratio KABHI nahi badalta.**

**Analogy:** Shadi me sharbat ke container se kisi ne bhi gilaas nikala, mithas same rahegi. Kyunki kuch bahar se add nahi hua.

Nikali hui quantity me bhi milk:water **wahi ratio** hota hai.

### Ex: Initial ratio 2 : 3. 50 L mixture nikala. Ratio same (2 : 3).

---

## 5. Nikaal + Pani Add (Combined Case)

### Q: Initial milk : water = 2 : 3. 50 L mixture nikala, phir 12 L water add kiya, ratio ban gaya 2 : 5. Initial mixture, aur final quantities?

**Step 1: Milk same** (kyunki pani add hua) → 2 = 2 ✔
**Step 2:** Water 3 → 5 = 2 units = 12 L → **1 unit = 6**
**Step 3 (Final):** Milk 2 × 6 = 12, Water 5 × 6 = 30, **Total = 42**
**Step 4 (Pani add hone se pehle):** milk 12, water 18 → mixture = **30 L** (ratio 2 : 3 → 5 units = 30)
**Step 5 (Nikaalne se pehle):** 30 + 50 = **80 L** (shuruaati mixture)
**Step 6 (Initial milk & water):** 80 L ka 2 : 3 → 1 unit = 16 → **Milk 32 L, Water 48 L**

**Dhyan:** 6 wali "unit" sirf **add wale hisse** ki hai. Initial wale hisse ko alag unit se nikalo.

### Q (Sanity check): 50 L nikala (2 : 3) → Milk 20, Water 30 ✔ (32 − 12 = 20, 48 − 18 = 30)

---

### Q: 90 L juice : water = 5 : 4. 36 L mixture nikala, same quantity water add kiya. Initial water : final water?
- Initial: 9 units = 90 → 1 unit = 10 → Juice 50, **Water 40**
- 36 L nikala → remaining = 54 L → (5 : 4) → 1 unit = 6 → Juice 30, Water 24
- 36 L pani add → Water = 24 + 36 = **60**
- Initial water : Final water = 40 : 60 = **2 : 3**

### Q: 100 L mixture milk : water = 7 : 3. 10 L nikala, x L water add kiya, naya ratio 21 : 11. 3x = ?
- 10 L nikala → 90 L bacha → Milk 63, Water 27
- Water add: 63 / (27 + x) = 21/11 → 27 + x = 33 → **x = 6**
- **3x = 18 L**

### Q: x L mixture (milk : water = 4 : 3) me se 49 L nikala aur 49 L water add kiya → ratio 2 : 5. Initial mixture?
- Milk same: 2 : 5 → ×2 → 4 : 10
- Water 3 → 10 = 7 units = 49 → 1 unit = 7
- Remaining mixture (nikalne ke baad) = (4 + 3) × 7 = **49 L**
- **Initial = 49 + 49 = 98 L**

### Q: Vessel me milk water = 3 : 2, milk water se 6 L zyada. 50% mixture nikala. Remaining milk?
- 1 unit = 6 L → Milk 18, Water 12
- 50% nikala → har ek ka 50% → **Milk = 9 L**

**Rule:** Mixture ka x% nikalne par har component ka x% nikalta hai.

## 6. "A is x% of B" — Base ka Confusion (Bahut Important!)

**Rule:** Jis se compare kiya ja raha hai (jo "of" ke baad hai) wo **base = 100** hota hai.

### Ex1: "Milk is 20% of Water"
- Water (base) = 100, Milk = 20 → ratio **1 : 5**

### Ex2: "Milk is 20% of Mixture"
- Mixture (base) = 100, Milk = 20, Water = 80 → ratio **1 : 4**

### Q: 120 L mixture, milk : water = 7 : 3. Kitna pani add karein ki milk resulting mixture ka 50% ho jaye?
- Milk 84 L, Water 36 L
- 50% milk → milk = water → 84 = 36 + x → **x = 48 L**

## 7. Replacement (Repeated Replacement) ⭐

**Situation:** Vessel me **pure milk** (ya alcohol) hai. Usme se kuch nikala, utna hi pani daala. Ye process kai baar repeat karna hai.

### Basic samajh (ek baar replace)
- 90 L pure milk, 10 L nikala → 80 L milk. 10 L pani add → **Milk : Water = 8 : 1**
- Container fir bhi 90 L ka hi hai (quantity same rahi), bas ab ye **mixture** hai.

### Dusri baar replace
- 90 L pure milk, 18 L nikala → Milk = 72, Water add 18 → **Milk : Water = 72 : 18 = 4 : 1**
- Dobara 18 L nikala (mixture, ratio 4 : 1): 72 L bacha → Milk = 57.6, Water = 14.4
- Water add 18 → Water = 32.4 → **Milk = 57.6, Water = 32.4**

### Shortcut Formula ⭐⭐

**Milk left (n replacements) = Initial × (1 − r/V)ⁿ**

jahan V = total quantity, r = har baar nikali hui quantity, n = kitni baar.

### Ex1: 90 L pure milk, 18 L nikaal ke pani, process 2 baar:
- Fraction = (90 − 18)/90 = 72/90 = **4/5**
- Milk = 90 × (4/5)² = 90 × 16/25 = **57.6 L**
- Water = 90 − 57.6 = **32.4 L**

### Ex2: Wahi, but "process **2 more times**" (total **3 baar**):
- Milk = 90 × (4/5)³ = 90 × 64/125 = **46.08 L**
- Water = 90 − 46.08 = **43.92 L**

**"More times" ka matlab:** "ek baar kiya, phir 2 baar aur" = **total 3 baar** (jab "more" nahi hai to utni hi baar).

## 8. Successive Replacement (alag-alag quantity)

### Q: 180 L pure milk. Pehle 18 L nikaal ke pani, phir 15 L mixture nikaal ke pani, phir 10 L mixture nikaal ke pani. Final milk?
- Milk = 180 × (162/180) × (165/180) × (170/180)
- = (162 × 165 × 170)/(180 × 180) = 4,544,100 / 32,400 = **140.25 L**
- (Water = 180 − 140.25 = 39.75 L)

**Dhyan:** Pehli baar sirf milk nikalta hai, baaki baar **mixture** nikalta hai. Isliye har baar fraction = (V − r)/V.

### Q: Vessel me pure alcohol. 6 L nikaal ke pani, 2 baar. Final alcohol : water = 100 : 69. Initial alcohol?
- Final total = 169 units (quantity same hi rahi)
- Alcohol fraction = (1 − 6/V)² = 100/169
- **Square root:** (1 − 6/V) = 10/13 → 6/V = 3/13 → **V = 26 L**
- Ratio trick: Initial 13 units → Final 10 units, 3 units = 6 L → 1 unit = 2 → **13 × 2 = 26 L**

## 9. Alloy Mixing (Copper–Zinc)

### Q1: Alloy A me copper : zinc = 3 : 4, Alloy B me 5 : 9. Barabar quantity mix karo. Naya ratio?

**Galat:** 3 + 5 = 8, 4 + 9 = 13 ❌
**Sahi:** Har alloy ka **fraction** lo
- A me: Copper = 3/7, Zinc = 4/7
- B me: Copper = 5/14, Zinc = 9/14
- Copper = 3/7 + 5/14 = 6/14 + 5/14 = **11/14**
- Zinc = 4/7 + 9/14 = 8/14 + 9/14 = **17/14**
- **Copper : Zinc = 11 : 17**

**Shortcut:** Dono alloys ke total ko **same** karo (7 aur 14 → A ko ×2): A = 6 : 8, B = 5 : 9 → Add → **11 : 17** ✔

### Q2: Wahi alloys, par A aur B ko 2 : 3 ratio me lo. Naya ratio?
- Copper = 2 × (3/7) + 3 × (5/14) = 6/7 + 15/14 = 12/14 + 15/14 = **27/14**
- Zinc = 2 × (4/7) + 3 × (9/14) = 8/7 + 27/14 = 16/14 + 27/14 = **43/14**
- **Copper : Zinc = 27 : 43**

# PART B – ALLIGATION

## 10. Alligation ka Concept

**Idea:** Do values (jaise cost price, speed, profit %) ko mila ke ek **mean (average) value** banti hai. Mean hamesha dono ke **beech** me hoti hai.

### Alligation Rule

Agar **Value 1 < Mean < Value 2**:

| | Value 1 (chhota) | Mean | Value 2 (bada) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diff | **(Value 2 − Mean)** | | **(Mean − Value 1)** |

**Ratio (Quantity 1 : Quantity 2) = (Value 2 − Mean) : (Mean − Value 1)**

**Yaad rakho:** Bade me se mean ghatao (ye chhote ka ratio hai), mean me se chhota ghatao (ye bade ka ratio hai). **Cross subtract.**

**Dhyan:** Mean ke dono taraf ki values ke difference ka ratio batata hai (ulte).

---

## 11. Ratio KIS cheez ka aayega? (Denominator Rule) ⭐

**Rule:** Alligation se jo ratio aata hai, wo us cheez ka hota hai jo **formula ke denominator me hoti hai.**

| Upar kya rakha | Formula | Ratio kiska aayega |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost price / rate (per kg)** | CP = ₹/kg | **Quantity (kg)** ka ratio |
| **Profit % / Loss %** (rupees ka mention ho) | Profit/CP × 100 | **CP** ka ratio |
| **Profit %** (rupees ka mention nahi) | | **Quantity** ka ratio |
| **Average** (marks, salary) | Sum/n | **Number of observations** (persons) ka ratio |
| **Speed** | D/T | **Time** ka ratio |
| **Discount %** | Discount/MRP × 100 | **MRP** ka ratio |
| **Interest %** | Interest/Principal × 100 | **Principal** ka ratio |
| **Bowling avg** (runs per wicket) | Runs/Wicket | **Wickets** ka ratio |
| **Percentage (population)** | x/Total × 100 | **Total (base)** ka ratio |

## 12. Solved Alligation Questions

### Q1: Town ki population 2,40,000. Males +7%, females +10%, total +9.5%. Initial males?
- Alligation: (10 − 9.5) : (9.5 − 7) = 0.5 : 2.5 = **1 : 5**
- Ratio **Males : Females = 1 : 5** (percentage → base/total population ka ratio)
- Males = 1/6 × 2,40,000 = **40,000**

### Q2: Do containers me alcohol : water = 3 : 1 aur 3 : 5. Kis ratio me mix karein ki alcohol : water = 5 : 3 ho?
- Alcohol fractions: 3/4, 3/8; Target 5/8
- Denominator 8 karo: 6/8, 3/8, mean = 5/8
- Alligation: (6 − 5) : (5 − 3) = **1 : 2**
- (Water se bhi try karo, answer same: 2/8, 5/8, mean 3/8 → (5 − 3) : (3 − 2) = 2 : 1; pehla container ke liye ratio ulta lagana hai (wahi 1 : 2) ✔)

### Q3: Income −5%, Expenditure −8%, Savings +4%. Income : Expenditure?
- **Rule:** Ek taraf Expenditure, ek taraf Savings, beech me Income (kyunki Income = Exp + Savings)
- Mean = −5, Exp = −8, Sav = +4
- Exp : Sav = (4 − (−5)) : (−5 − (−8)) = 9 : 3 = **3 : 1**
- Income = 3 + 1 = 4 → **Income : Expenditure = 4 : 3**

### Q4: ₹80,000 do banks me. Bank 1: 9.5%, Bank 2: 5.5%. Total interest ₹6400. Bank 1 me kitna?
- Overall % = 6400/80000 × 100 = **8%**
- Ratio (Principal): (8 − 5.5) : (9.5 − 8) = 2.5 : 1.5 = **5 : 3**
- Bank 1 = 5/8 × 80,000 = **₹50,000**

### Q5: Do rice (₹60/kg aur ₹64/kg) kis ratio me mix karein ki ₹68.20/kg par bechne se 10% profit ho?
- **Mean CP chahiye, SP nahi!** SP = 110% of CP → CP = 68.20 × 100/110 = **₹62**
- Alligation: (64 − 62) : (62 − 60) = 2 : 2 = **1 : 1**

### Q6: Boxes (4% profit) aur pens (7% profit), overall profit 6%. Total CP ₹300. Boxes = 20, Pens = 16. Cost per box and pen?
- Alligation: (7 − 6) : (6 − 4) = **1 : 2** (profit% ke saath rupees → **CP ka ratio**)
- Boxes CP = 100, Pens CP = 200
- Box = 100/20 = **₹5**; Pen = 200/16 = **₹12.50**

### Q7: 700 employees. Males avg ₹4200, Females avg ₹4100. Overall avg ₹4190. Females?
- (4190 − 4100) : (4200 − 4190) = 90 : 10 = **9 : 1** (Males : Females) — average → **number of persons**
- Females = 1/10 × 700 = **70**

### Q8: Ek aadmi 20 km/h se 30 min, y km/h se 45 min chala. Avg speed 14 km/h. y = ?
- Speed → **time** ka ratio
- 20 km/h ke liye time 30, y ke liye 45 → Time ratio (y : 20) = 45 : 30 = **3 : 2**
- Alligation: (20 − 14) : (14 − y) = 6 : (14 − y) = 3 : 2 → 14 − y = 4 → **y = 10 km/h**

### Q9: Seller do horses sells. Total MRP ₹4200. Do discounts alag-alag, total discount 12% (gaps 4 aur 3). First horse ka MRP?
- Discount → **MRP** ka ratio
- Ratio = **3 : 4** (total 7 units = 4200 → 1 unit = 600)
- **First horse MRP = 3 × 600 = ₹1800**

### Q10: Bowler ka avg 11.5 runs/wicket. Agle innings me 5 wickets, 45 runs (avg 9). Overall avg 5 kam hokar 11 ho gaya. Pehle ke wickets?
- Bowling avg → **wickets** ka ratio
- Mean = 11; Old avg = 11.5, New avg = 9
- Alligation: Old : New = (11 − 9) : (11.5 − 11) = 2 : 0.5 = **4 : 1**
- New wickets = 5 → **Old wickets = 20**

## 13. Quick Revision Cheat-Sheet

### Mixture
| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Pani add | **Milk constant** rakho, pani ka difference unit |
| Nikalna | **Ratio same** rehta hai |
| Nikaal + add | Pehle add wala hissa solve, phir peeche aao |
| "A is x% of B" | B ko 100 maano |
| Replacement (pure se) | Milk = Initial × (1 − r/V)ⁿ |
| Successive replacement | Fractions multiply |
| Alloys | Pehle fractions, ya totals same karo |

### Alligation
| Cheez | Formula |
|---|---|
| Alligation | (High − Mean) : (Mean − Low) |
| Ratio kiska | Denominator ka (CP→quantity; speed→time; avg→persons; discount→MRP; profit%→CP) |

## 14. Common Mistakes

1. **Pani add hone par milk ka ratio badal dena** — milk ko barabar karo.
2. **Nikalne par ratio badal dena** — ratio same rehta hai.
3. **1 unit ko galat hisse me use karna** — old aur new alag-alag units hote hain (gate/darwaza concept).
4. **Mixture puchha ho to sirf milk de dena** — total (sum) puchha hai.
5. **"More times" ka matlab bhoolna** — 1 + n more = n + 1 baar.
6. **Replacement formula me V ya r galat** — V = total, r = har baar nikali hui quantity.
7. **Alligation me SP ko mean maan lena** — Profit wale case me **CP** mean hota hai.
8. **Ratio kiska hai ye bhoolna** — denominator wali cheez dekho.
9. **Alloy ko 3 + 5, 4 + 9 add karna** — fractions lena zaroori hai.
10. **Alligation me cross subtract bhoolna** — bade me se mean, mean me se chhota.

## Study Tips (Sir ki advice)

- Pehle **basic rules** (add, remove) pakke karo, phir replacement.
- **Alligation ko tab lagao jab dikhe ki do cheezein mil rahi hain** aur average/mean value di hai.
- Hamesha pooch lo: "Ye ratio kis cheez ka hai?" (denominator rule).
- Practice: har question me **gate/darwaza** (old & new) concept dhyan rakho.

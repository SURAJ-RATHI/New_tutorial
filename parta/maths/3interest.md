## 1. Interest kya hota hai?

**Example:** Tumne dost se ₹100 liye, 1 saal baad ₹110 wapas karne hain.

| Term | Matlab | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Principal (P)** | Jitna udhaar liya (Mooldhan) | ₹100 |
| **Amount (A)** | Wapas kitna dena hai (Mishradhan) | ₹110 |
| **Interest (I)** | Extra paisa jo dena pada (Byaaj) | ₹10 |
| **Time (T)** | Kitna time diya | 1 year |
| **Rate (R)** | Interest ka % | 10% |

**Formulas:**
- **Amount = Principal + Interest** (A = P + I)
- **Rate % = (Interest / Principal) × 100** → ₹10 / ₹100 × 100 = 10%

**Definition (exam ke liye):** Interest = **"Extra money to be paid"**.
(Sirf "byaaj" mat bolna, wo to Hindi meaning hai, definition nahi.)

---

## 2. Simple Interest vs Compound Interest

| | Simple Interest (SI) | Compound Interest (CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Kis par charge hota hai | **Sirf Principal** par, har saal | **Principal + ab tak mila interest**, har saal |
| Hindi | Saadharan byaaj | Chakravriddhi byaaj |
| Har saal interest | **Same** rehta hai | **Badhta** jata hai |

**Yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** CI = "byaaj par bhi byaaj" (lalchi interest 😄).

# PART A – SIMPLE INTEREST

## 3.1 Formula

**SI = (P × R × T) / 100**

Lekin simple cases me formula ki jagah **percentage method** zyada fast hai.

### Percentage Method (Sabse fast)

**Total interest % = Rate × Time** (kyunki har saal same % milta hai)
Phir Principal ka wo % nikalo.

**Examples:**

| P | R | T | Rate × Time | SI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹1000 | 10% | 3 yr | 30% | 30% of 1000 = **₹300** |
| ₹5000 | 20% | 4 yr | 80% | 80% of 5000 = **₹4000** |
| ₹1500 | 30% | 5 yr | 150% | 150% of 1500 = **₹2250** |

### Fraction wale rates (percentage chapter ka use!)

Mixed % ko fraction me badlo. Common fractions yaad rakho:
- 16⅔% = **1/6**
- 14²⁄₇% = **1/7**
- 33⅓% = **1/3**
- 6⅔% = **1/15**
- 12.5% = **1/8**

**Ex1:** ₹1200, 16⅔% (=1/6), 10 years
- SI = 1200 × (1/6) × 10 = **₹2000**

**Ex2:** ₹1400, 14²⁄₇% (=1/7), 6 years
- SI = 1400 × (1/7) × 6 = **₹1200**
- **Warning:** Formula me R/100 ki jagah poora "1/7" lagana hai (kyunki 1/7 hi R/100 ki value hai). Dobara /100 mat karna!

---

## 3.2 SI ke Scaling Rules (Important!)

- Agar **P aur T same** hain aur **Rate n guna** hota hai → **SI bhi n guna**
  (₹1000, 10%→20%→30% par SI = 100 → 200 → 300)
- Agar **R aur T same** hain aur **Principal n guna** hota hai → **SI bhi n guna**

### Q: 10 saal ka SI ₹1500 hai. 5 saal baad principal 3 guna ho jata hai. Total SI?
- 1 saal ka SI = 1500/10 = ₹150
- Pehle 5 saal: 150 × 5 = ₹750
- Agle 5 saal (principal ×3): 750 × 3 = ₹2250
- **Total = 750 + 2250 = ₹3000**

---

## 3.3 Amount vs Principal – Keyword Trick

- **"amounts to"**, **"becomes"**, **"grows to"**, **"doubles/triples"** → ye **Amount** ki baat hai
- Sirf **"sum"** ya **"principal"** ya "money lent/invested" → **Principal**

---

## 3.4 "Money Doubles / Triples" Type

**Concept:** Agar P = 1 maan lo to Amount bataya gaya hai, Interest = Amount − P.

### Q1: 10% rate, 10 years, SI = ₹2000. Amount : Principal ratio?
- Interest % = 10 × 10 = **100%** of P
- Amount = P + SI = 100% + 100% = **200%**
- **A : P = 2 : 1**

### Q2: Koi sum 4 saal me 3 guna ho jata hai. Rate?
- P = ₹1 → A = ₹3 → SI = ₹2 (4 saal me)
- 1 saal ka SI = 2/4 = ₹0.5
- Rate = (1 year SI / P) × 100 = 0.5/1 × 100 = **50%**

**Formula:** R = (1 saal ka SI / P) × 100

### Q3: Sum 6 saal me double hota hai. Kitne saal me 5 guna hoga?
- P = 1 → double = 2 → SI = 1 (6 saal me)
- 5 guna → SI = 4
- ₹1 jodne me 6 saal → ₹4 jodne me **4 × 6 = 24 years**

---

## 3.5 Amount se Principal – Same Time Rule (Bahut Important!)

**Rule:** **P = A − SI** tabhi sahi hai jab **dono ka time SAME ho.**

**Galat example:** A (2 saal ka) = ₹5000, SI (1 saal ka) = ₹1000 → P = 4000 ❌
**Sahi:** 1 saal SI = 1000 → 2 saal ka SI = 2000 → P = 5000 − 2000 = **₹3000** ✔

### Q: Koi sum 1 saal me ₹600 aur 4 saal me ₹900 ho jata hai (SI par). Sum aur rate?
- 1 saal → 4 saal = 3 saal me **₹300** jude
- 1 saal ka SI = 300/3 = **₹100**
- **Method 1:** A(1 yr) − SI(1 yr) = 600 − 100 = **₹500** = P
- **Method 2:** A(4 yr) − SI(4 yr) = 900 − 400 = **₹500** ✔
- Rate = (100/500) × 100 = **20%**

### Q: Koi sum 3 saal me ₹14,880 aur 5 saal me ₹16,800 ho jata hai. Sum?
- 3 → 5 saal (2 saal) me jude: 16,800 − 14,880 = **₹1920**
- 1 saal ka SI = 960
- 3 saal ka SI = 960 × 3 = 2880
- P = 14,880 − 2880 = **₹12,000**

---

## 3.6 Alag-alag Rates alag-alag time par

### Q: Ramu ne sum lend ki: 4% pehle 3 saal, 8% agle 4 saal, 12% 7 saal ke baad. 11 saal me SI = ₹27,600. Sum?
- 3 yr × 4% = **12%**
- 4 yr × 8% = **32%**
- Baaki 4 yr × 12% = **48%**
- Total = 12 + 32 + 48 = **92%** of P
- 92% = 27,600 → 1% = 300 → P (100%) = **₹30,000**

---

## 3.7 Ratio / Distribution Questions

### Q: ₹18,000 teen parts me 3%, 4%, 6% par invest kiya (1 saal). Teeno se interest equal mila. 6% wali amount?
- Equal interest → investment **inverse of rate**
- Ratio = 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/6 → LCM 12 se multiply → **4 : 3 : 2**
- (Hide & Seek trick: x chhupao → baaki multiply: 4×6=24, 3×6=18, 2×... jo bhi ho, simplify karke 4:3:2)
- 6% wali (ratio 2) = 18,000 × 2/9 = **₹4000**

---

## 3.8 Age-based Question (Mains Level)

### Q: ₹29,000 do bete (age 9 aur 13) ke liye invest kiya, 4.5% SI par, aise ki 21 saal ke hone par dono ko **equal amount** mile. Chhote ka share?

- Chhota (9 yr): 21 − 9 = **12 years** ka interest
- Bada (13 yr): 21 − 13 = **8 years** ka interest
- Chhote ka interest % = 4.5 × 12 = **54%** → Amount = **154%** of x
- Bade ka interest % = 4.5 × 8 = **36%** → Amount = **136%** of y
- Equal amount: 154% of x = 136% of y
- x : y = 136 : 154 = **68 : 77**
- Chhote ka share = 29000 × 68/145 = 200 × 68 = **₹13,600**

**Lesson:** Time = (jab paisa milega − abhi ki age). Aur "equal **amount**" hai, "equal **interest**" nahi — isliye 100 add karna padta hai.

# PART B – COMPOUND INTEREST

## 4.1 CI ka Concept (Dekho kaise hota hai)

**Ex:** P = ₹1000, R = 10%, T = 2 years
- Year 1 interest: 10% of 1000 = ₹100
- Year 2 interest: 10% of 1000 = ₹100 **+ 10% of pichhla interest (100) = ₹10**
- Total CI = 100 + 100 + 10 = **₹210**

**Ex:** P = ₹2000, R = 20%, T = 2 years
- Year 1: 400
- Year 2: 400 + (20% of 400 = 80)
- CI = 400 + 400 + 80 = **₹880**

---

## 4.2 Slab Method (Sabse popular, mental calc ke liye best)

**Idea:** Har saal ke interest ko "layers" me likho. Pattern **binomial / Pascal's triangle** jaisa chalta hai.

### 2 Years: Pattern **1 : 2 : 1**
- Level 1: Principal ka R% (jo har saal hota hai) → **× 2** (2 saal)
- Level 2: Level 1 ka R% → **× 1**

**Ex:** ₹25,000, 20%, 2 yr
- Level 1: 20% of 25,000 = 5000 → ×2 = **10,000**
- Level 2: 20% of 5000 = 1000 → ×1 = **1000**
- **CI = 11,000** ✔

### 3 Years: Pattern **1 : 3 : 3 : 1**
- Level 1 (P ka R%) → **× 3**
- Level 2 (Level 1 ka R%) → **× 3**
- Level 3 (Level 2 ka R%) → **× 1**

**Ex 1:** ₹8000, 10%, 3 yr
- L1 = 800 → ×3 = 2400
- L2 = 80 → ×3 = 240
- L3 = 8 → ×1 = 8
- **CI = 2400 + 240 + 8 = ₹2648**

**Ex 2:** ₹2160, 16⅔% (1/6), 3 yr
- L1 = 360 → ×3 = 1080
- L2 = 60 → ×3 = 180
- L3 = 10 → ×1 = 10
- **CI = ₹1270**

**Ex 3:** ₹33,750, 6⅔% (1/15), 3 yr
- L1 = 2250 → ×3 = 6750
- L2 = 150 → ×3 = 450
- L3 = 10 → ×1 = 10
- **CI = ₹7210**

> Jab percentage "nice" fraction hota hai (10%, 20%, 1/6, 1/15, 1/3) tab slab method bahut fast hai.

---

## 4.3 Chain Approach

**Idea:** Rate ko fraction banao → **Amount multiplier** = (1 + fraction).
Har saal me P ko isi multiplier se multiply karo.

| Rate | Fraction | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| 20% | 1/5 | **6/5** |
| 10% | 1/10 | **11/10** |
| 16⅔% | 1/6 | **7/6** |
| 12.5% | 1/8 | **9/8** |
| 6⅔% | 1/15 | **16/15** |
| 33⅓% | 1/3 | **4/3** |
| 8% | 2/25 | **27/25** |

**Formula:** **Amount = P × (multiplier)^T**, phir **CI = Amount − P**

**Ex 1:** ₹2000, 20%, 2 yr
- A = 2000 × (6/5)² = 2000 × 36/25 = **₹2880**
- CI = 2880 − 2000 = **₹880**

**Ex 2:** ₹2160, 1/6, 3 yr
- A = 2160 × (7/6)³ = 2160 × 343/216 = **₹3430**
- CI = 3430 − 2160 = **₹1270** ✔ (slab se bhi yehi aaya)

---

## 4.4 Successive Approach (Ratio method – Principal : Amount)

**Idea:** Per saal P : A ka ratio likho, jitne saal utni baar multiply karo.

**Ex:** ₹33,750, 6⅔% (1/15), 3 yr
- Ek saal: P : A = 15 : 16
- 3 saal: 15³ : 16³ = **3375 : 4096**
- CI ratio = 4096 − 3375 = **721**
- 3375 units = 33,750 → 1 unit = 10
- CI = 721 × 10 = **₹7210** ✔

**Ex:** ₹8000, 10%, 3 yr
- P : A = 10 : 11 → 10³ : 11³ = **1000 : 1331**
- CI = 331 units; 1000 units = 8000 → 1 unit = 8
- CI = 331 × 8 = **₹2648** ✔

---

## 4.5 Fractional Time (jaise 2¼ years)

**Method:** Poore saalon ka CI lo + bacha hua part ka **simple fraction** (pichhle/agle saal ke interest ka hissa).

### Q: ₹2700, 33⅓% (1/3), 2¼ years. CI?
- Slab (3 yr): L1 = 900, L2 = 300, L3 = 100
- Year 1 CI = 900
- Year 2 CI = 900 + 300 = 1200
- Year 3 CI = 900 + 300 + 300 + 100 = 1600
- 2 saal ka CI = 900 + 1200 = **2100**
- Baaki ¼ saal = **¼ × (3rd year ka interest 1600) = 400**
- **Total CI = 2100 + 400 = ₹2500**

(Check: 2 saal baad Amount = 2700 × 16/9 = 4800. ¼ saal ka SI on 4800 at 33⅓% = 4800 × 1/3 × ¼ = 400 ✔)

---

## 4.6 Specific Year ka CI (Sirf ek saal ka)

**Rule:** Agar kisi saal ka CI pata ho to **agle saal ka CI = us CI × (1 + R/100)** (yaani usi chain multiplier se).

### Ex: ₹1000, 20%, 3 years
- Year 1 CI = 200
- Year 2 CI = 200 × 6/5 = **240**
- Year 3 CI = 240 × 6/5 = **288**
- Total 3 yr CI = 200 + 240 + 288 = **728** ✔

### Q: 2nd year ka CI = ₹200, 3rd year ka CI = ₹210. Rate?
- Increase = 10 on 200 = **5%** ✔

(Isi ka matlab: agle saal ka CI − pichhle saal ka CI = pichhle saal ke CI par ek saal ka interest.)

### Q: 4th year ka CI = ₹6250, rate 8%. 5th year ka CI?
- 8% = 2/25 → multiplier 27/25
- 5th year = 6250 × 27/25 = **₹6750**

### Q: 1st year ka CI = ₹6400, rate 12.5% (1/8). 4th year ka CI?
- Multiplier 9/8, teen baar (1st → 4th)
- 4th year CI = 6400 × (9/8)³ = 6400 × 729/512 = **₹9112.50**

### Q: P = ₹6250, 20%, 4th year ka CI?
- 1st year CI = 20% of 6250 = 1250
- 4th year = 1250 × (6/5)³ = 1250 × 216/125 = **₹2160**

### Q: ₹5000, 10%. 2nd aur 3rd year ke CI me difference?
- Slab: 2nd year interest = 500 + 50 = 550; 3rd year = 500 + 50 + 50 + 5 = 605
- Difference = **₹55**
- Shortcut: 2nd year CI (550) par 10% = **55** ✔

## 4.7 Special Types

### 4.7.1 Rate nikalna jab P aur A diye ho
**Steps:**
1. P : A ratio simplify karo.
2. Time ke hisab se **root** lo (2 saal → square root, 3 saal → cube root).
3. Jo ratio mile (jaise 20 : 21), usse **(increase/original) × 100** karo.

**Q:** ₹6400, A = ₹7056 (2 saal). Rate?
- 6400 : 7056 = 400 : 441 (÷16)
- √ → 20 : 21
- Rate = (1/20) × 100 = **5%**

### 4.7.2 Do alag-alag time ka Amount diya ho
**Q:** Koi sum 3 saal me ₹7800, 5 saal me ₹11,232 ho jata hai (CI). Rate?
- 3 → 5 saal = **2 saal ka journey**
- Yahan "7800" ko principal maano, "11232" ko amount, time = 2 yr
- Ratio 7800 : 11232 = **25 : 36**
- √ → **5 : 6** → increase 1 on 5 → **20%**

### 4.7.3 Triple / "n times" type (Time nikalna)
**Concept:** Agar x years me k guna hota hai aur hame y guna dena hai, to **powers ko compare karo (same base)**.

**Q:** Sum 10 years me 3 guna hota hai (CI). Kitne saal me **243 guna** hoga?
- 243 = **3⁵**, 3 = **3¹**
- 1 power ↔ 10 years → 5 power ↔ **10 × 5 = 50 years**

**Rule:** (Known time) × (new power / old power) = New time.

## 4.8 Half-yearly aur Quarterly Compounding

| Compounding | Rate | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Half-yearly** | **R ÷ 2** | **T × 2** |
| **Quarterly** | **R ÷ 4** | **T × 4** |

**Ex:** R = 20% p.a., T = 2 yr, half-yearly → R = 10%, T = 4
**Ex:** R = 20% p.a., T = 2 yr, quarterly → R = 5%, T = 8

Baaki saara kuch (slab/chain/successive) same hi hai.

---

## 4.9 Effective Rate (SI vs CI)

**2 years ke liye:** Effective % = **a + b + (ab/100)** (a = b = rate)

| Rate | SI (2 yr) | CI (2 yr) |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 10 + 10 = **20%** | 10 + 10 + (100/100) = **21%** |
| 20% | 20 + 20 = **40%** | 20 + 20 + (400/100) = **44%** |

**Use:** ₹6000, 20%, 2 yr CI = 44% of 6000 = **₹2640** (seedha!)

**3 years ke liye:** pehle 2 saal ka effective rate (21%) nikalo, phir usse 1 saal wale rate (10%) ke saath combine karo.
- 21 + 10 + (21 × 10)/100 = 31 + 2.1 = **33.1%**

---

## 4.10 CI − SI ka Difference (Formulas)

**2 years:**

**CI − SI = P × (R/100)²** (jise likhte hain PR²/100²)

**3 years:**

**CI − SI = P × R²(300 + R) / 100³**

*(Ye formulas sirf is type ke "difference" questions ke liye. Time 2 saal ya 3 saal hona chahiye.)*

## 6. Common Mistakes

1. **"Interest" ki definition me sirf "byaaj" likhna** — ye meaning hai, definition nahi. "Extra money to be paid" likho.
2. **R/100 ki jagah R ke fraction me phir se /100 lagana** (jaise 1/7 ko dobara 100 se divide).
3. **Amount aur SI ka time alag hone par subtract karna** (A − SI tabhi jab time same ho).
4. **"Equal amount" aur "equal interest" ko mix karna** — equal amount me Principal ka 100% add karna padta hai.
5. **Question ke last word ko ignore karna** (principal puchha tha, amount nikal diya, ya CI puchha tha, Amount likh diya).
6. **Specific-year CI aur total CI ko mix karna** — "2nd year ka CI" ≠ "2 saal ka CI".
7. **Slab method me pattern bhoolna** — 2 yr = 1:2:1, 3 yr = 1:3:3:1.
8. **Half-yearly/quarterly me sirf rate ya sirf time badalna** — dono badalna padta hai.
9. **Fractional time (2¼ yrs) me poora 3rd saal le lena** — sirf ¼ part lena hai.

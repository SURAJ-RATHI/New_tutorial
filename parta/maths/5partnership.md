## 1. Partnership kya hota hai?

- Hindi: **Sajhedari**
- Jab **do ya do se zyada log** milkar koi **business** start karte hain, to use partnership kehte hain.

**Pura chapter 3 steps me:**
1. Business start karo (sabka investment)
2. Profit aayega
3. Profit ko **sahi ratio me share** karo

## 2. Golden Formula (Sabse Important)

**Profit Share ∝ Money × Time**

**Kyun?** Sirf paisa kitna lagaya wo kaafi nahi, **kitne time ke liye** lagaya wo bhi matter karta hai (SIP ki tarah: jitna long-term invest, utna zyada fayda).

**Example:** A ne ₹100, 3 saal ke liye; B ne ₹200, 1 saal ke liye.
- A: 100 × 3 = 300
- B: 200 × 1 = 200
- **Profit ratio = 3 : 2** (A ka hissa zyada, bhale hi B ne paisa zyada lagaya)

> Is formula par pura chapter based hai.

## 3. Profit ko Ratio me Distribute Karna

### Method 1 (Unit method)
**₹500 ko A aur B me 2 : 3 ke ratio me baantna hai.**
- Total ratio = 2 + 3 = 5 units = ₹500
- 1 unit = ₹100
- A = 2 × 100 = **₹200**, B = 3 × 100 = **₹300**

### Method 2 (Fraction method)
- A = 2/5 × 500 = **200**
- B = 3/5 × 500 = **300**

**Rule:** Jiska share chahiye, uska ratio-part upar, total ratio neeche, aur total amount se multiply.

### 3 logon ka example
**₹2700 ko A : B : C = 2 : 3 : 4 me baantna hai.**
- Total ratio = 9 units = ₹2700 → 1 unit = ₹300
- A = 2 × 300 = **₹600**
- B = 3 × 300 = **₹900**
- C = 4 × 300 = **₹1200**

## 4. Basic Questions

### Q1: A ne ₹200, 5 saal ke liye. B ne ₹600, 3 saal ke liye. Profit ratio?
- A = 200 × 5 = 1000
- B = 600 × 3 = 1800
- Ratio = 1000 : 1800 = **5 : 9**

### Q2: A ne ₹500, 2 saal ke liye. B ne ₹1500, 3 saal ke liye. Total profit ₹4400. Dono ka share?
- A = 500 × 2 = 1000
- B = 1500 × 3 = 4500
- Ratio = 1000 : 4500 = **2 : 9** (total 11)
- A = 2/11 × 4400 = **₹800**
- B = 9/11 × 4400 = **₹3600**

### Q3 (Teen partners): A = ₹200 (3 yr), B = ₹300 (4 yr), C = ₹400 (5 yr). Profit ratio?
- A = 200 × 3 = 600
- B = 300 × 4 = 1200
- C = 400 × 5 = 2000
- Ratio = 600 : 1200 : 2000 = **3 : 6 : 10**

## 5. Unknown Value (x ya t) Nikalna

**Idea:** Jahan koi value nahi pata (x), wahan investment × time me **x** likho, aur jo **profit ratio question me diya hai**, uske saath **equate** karo (dono profit ratios hain isliye barabar).

### Q1: A = ₹500 (2 yr), B = ₹x (3 yr). Profit ratio A : B = 2 : 9. Find x.
- A = 500 × 2 = 1000; B = 3x
- 1000 / 3x = 2/9
- 3x × 2 = 9000 → 3x = 4500 → **x = ₹1500**

**Shortcut:** Apne ratio (1000 : 3x) ko diye hue ratio (2 : 9) se divide karo, beech me "=" lagao.
- 1000 ÷ 2 = 500 aur 3x ÷ 9 → 500 = x/3 → x = 1500 ✔

### Q2: A = ₹200 (3 yr), B = ₹300 (t yr), C = ₹400 (5 yr). Profit ratio 3 : 6 : 10. Find t.
- A = 600, B = 300t, C = 2000
- Ratio 600 : 300t : 2000 = 6 : 3t : 20 (÷100)
- Given ratio 3 : 6 : 10 → ×2 → 6 : 12 : 20
- 3t = 12 → **t = 4 years**

**Tip:** Kisi bhi ek partner se equate kar sakte ho (A se, B se, ya C se), answer same aayega.

## 6. Entry Concept (Beech me paisa add karna)

**Rule:** Jitne time tak **purana amount** laga, usko pehle multiply karo. Phir **naya amount** (purana + add kiya) bacha hua time se multiply karo.

**Steps:**
1. Pehle **total business time** dekho (jaise 1 saal = 12 months).
2. Jab paisa add hua, tab tak purana amount laga.
3. Add hone ke baad naya total amount bacha hua time ke liye.
4. Jiska time nahi diya wo **poore time** ke liye maana jayega (understood).

### Q1: A = ₹1500, B = ₹2000. 4 mahine baad A ne ₹500 aur add kiya. Total business 1 saal. Profit ratio?
- Total time = 12 months
- A: 1500 × 4 + (1500 + 500) × 8 = 6000 + 16000 = **22000**
- B (time nahi diya → poora 12 months): 2000 × 12 = **24000**
- Ratio = 22000 : 24000 = **11 : 12**

**Analogy (Sir ki):** Dost 3 ghante baad aaye to shuru ke 3 ghante aapne akele jhele. Waise hi naya paisa aane se pehle purana paisa hi kaam karta hai.

### Q2: A = ₹2500, B = ₹3500. 3 mahine baad A ne ₹500 add kiya. Uske **3 mahine aur baad** B ne ₹500 add kiya. Total 1 saal. Profit ratio?
- Total time = 12 months
- **A:** 3 months ke liye 2500, baaki 9 months ke liye (2500 + 500 = 3000)
  → 2500 × 3 + 3000 × 9 = 7500 + 27000 = **34500**
- **B:** "3 months more" matlab **3 + 3 = 6 months** baad aaya.
  6 months ke liye 3500, baaki 6 months ke liye (3500 + 500 = 4000)
  → 3500 × 6 + 4000 × 6 = 21000 + 24000 = **45000**
- Ratio = 34500 : 45000 = **23 : 30**

## 7. Withdrawal Concept (Beech me paisa nikalna)

**Rule:** Entry ke ulta: nikalne ke baad **amount kam** ho jata hai.

### Q: A = ₹4000, B = ₹3000. Total business 18 months. 8 months baad A ne ₹1000 nikaal liye. 12 months baad B ne ₹1000 add kiye. Profit ratio?
- **A:** 4000 × 8 + (4000 − 1000) × 10 = 32000 + 30000 = **62000**
- **B:** 3000 × 12 + (3000 + 1000) × 6 = 36000 + 18000... (4000 × 6 = 24000) → 36000 + 24000 = **60000**
- Ratio = 62000 : 60000 = **31 : 30**

**Note:** Yahan total time 12 nahi, **18 months** hai. Isliye bacha hua time = 18 − 8 = 10, aur 18 − 12 = 6.

---

## 8. "After x more months" ka Matlab

- **"After 3 months, A added. After 3 more months, B added"** → B **6 months** (3 + 3) baad aaya (cumulative).
- Sirf **"after 12 months"** likha hai (more nahi) → seedha **12 months** baad.

> **"more"** word ka matlab = pichle time me aur jodo.

---

## 9. "Garib Banao" Approach (Shortcut)

**Idea:** Investment ko **chhota** kar do, taaki calculation easy ho.

**Rules:**
1. Sabke investment se **common zeros / common factor** hata do (jaise sabme 3 zero hai to 3 hata do; agar kisi me kam hain to jitne sabme common ho utne hi).
2. **Sirf investment ke saath ye karte hain.** **Profit ke saath kabhi nahi!** (₹1200 profit ke zero hataoge to ₹12 ho jayega, galat!)

**Example:** A = 4000, B = 3000, Add/withdraw = 1000
→ Garib banao (3 zero hata do): A = 4, B = 3, change = 1
- A: 4 × 8 + 3 × 10 = 32 + 30 = 62
- B: 3 × 12 + 4 × 6 = 36 + 24 = 60
- Ratio = 31 : 30 ✔ (same answer)

## 10. "x months before" Ka Confusion

**Rule:** Agar partner **business khatam hone se x months pehle** chhod de, to uska time = **Total − x**.

**Examples:** Business 12 months ka:
- 8 months pehle chhoda → time = 12 − 8 = **4 months**
- 9 months pehle chhoda → time = 12 − 9 = **3 months**
- x months pehle chhoda → time = **12 − x**

**Aur agar "x months baad aaya"** → time = Total − x.

## 11. "Out of" Type (Fav. in exams ⭐)

### Q1: A aur B ne 3 : 5 ratio me invest kiya. 6 months baad C ne B ke investment ka 3 guna lekar join kiya. Saal ke end me total profit ₹6200. A ka share?
- Total time = 12 months
- A = 3 (12 months), B = 5 (12 months), C = 15 (kyunki 3 × B = 15) (6 months)
- A = 3 × 12 = 36; B = 5 × 12 = 60; C = 15 × 6 = 90
- Ratio = 36 : 60 : 90 = **6 : 10 : 15** (total 31)
- A's share = 6/31 × 6200 = 6 × 200 = **₹1200**

### Q2 (x months ka): A = ₹12,000, B = ₹12,000 (sirf x months ke liye), C = ₹8,000 (jisne saal khatam hone se x months pehle chhod diya). Total annual profit ₹3200 me se A ko ₹1800 mile. x = ?

**Step 1 (Garib banao, 4000 se divide):** A = 3, B = 3, C = 2 (thousand units ... ya seedha ratio banao)
- A = 12 × 12 = 144
- B = 12 × x = 12x
- C = 8 × (12 − x) [x months pehle chhodne par time = 12 − x]
- Sabko 4 se divide: **36 : 3x : 2(12 − x)**

**Step 2:** A ko ₹1800 → baaki (B + C) ko ₹1400
- **A : (B + C) = 1800 : 1400 = 9 : 7**

**Step 3:** Equation
- 36 / [3x + 2(12 − x)] = 9/7
- 3x + 24 − 2x = x + 24
- 36 / (x + 24) = 9/7
- 36 × 7 = 9(x + 24)
- 28 = x + 24 → **x = 4 months**

> **Tip:** "Out of total profit" wale questions ko pehle practice kar lena. Ye bahut baar aate hain.

## 12. Increase / Decrease by % Wale Questions

### Q: Rahul ne ₹x, Vikas ne ₹7000 invest kiye. 5 months baad Rahul ne apna investment **50%** badha diya. Vikas ne **7 months** baad apna investment **30%** ghata diya. Total business 12 months. Rahul ka share ₹1550, total profit ₹3020. x = ?

**Step 1: Rahul**
- 5 months ke liye x
- Baaki 7 months ke liye x × 3/2 = **3x/2** (50% increase = 3/2 se multiply)
- Rahul = 5x + 7 × (3x/2) = 5x + 21x/2 = **31x/2**

**Step 2: Vikas**
- 7 months ke liye ₹7000
- Baaki 5 months ke liye 7000 − 30% of 7000 = 7000 − 2100 = **₹4900**
- Vikas = 7000 × 7 + 4900 × 5 = 49000 + 24500 = **73500**

**Step 3: Profit split**
- Total profit = 3020; Rahul = 1550 → Vikas = 3020 − 1550 = **1470**
- Profit ratio = 1550 : 1470 = **155 : 147**

**Step 4: Equate**
- (31x/2) : 73500 = 155 : 147
- 31x : 147000 = 155 : 147 (dono side ×2)
- 31x ÷ 155 = 147000 ÷ 147 → 31x/155 = 1000 → 31x = 155000
- **x = ₹5000**

**Conversion yaad rakho:**
- x% increase → multiply (100 + x)/100 (50% → 3/2)
- x% decrease → multiply (100 − x)/100 (30% → 7/10)

## 13. Difference of Profit Shares

### Q: A, B, C ka investment ratio 12 : 5 : 7 (₹48,000 : ₹20,000 : ₹28,000 ko 4000 se divide karke), time sabka same (1 saal). B ka profit ₹3500. A aur C ke profit me difference?

- Time same hai → ignore (12/12 cancel)
- Profit ratio = 12 : 5 : 7
- B = 5 units = ₹3500 → 1 unit = ₹700
- A − C = 12 − 7 = **5 units** = 5 × 700 = **₹3500**
  (Pehle se B ke 5 units diye the, to calculation karne ki zarurat hi nahi thi, 5 ratio = 3500.)

> **Lesson:** Jab time sabka same ho, to time likhne ki zarurat nahi (sirf investment ratio use karo).

## 14. Quick Revision Cheat-Sheet

| Topic | Key Trick |
|---|---|
| Profit share | Money × Time |
| Same time | Sirf investment ka ratio |
| Same money | Sirf time ka ratio |
| Distribution | Unit method / fraction method |
| Unknown x | Profit ratio se equate (diye hue ratio se) |
| Beech me add | Purana × pehle time + Naya × baaki time |
| Beech me nikala | Ghata hua amount × baaki time |
| "After x more months" | Cumulative (3 + 3 = 6) |
| "x months before end" | Time = Total − x |
| Garib banao | Sirf investment se common zeros hatao, profit se nahi |
| Time nahi diya | Poora business time maano |
| x% increase | × (100 + x)/100 |
| x% decrease | × (100 − x)/100 |
| "Out of total profit" | A : (B + C) ka ratio banao, equation solve |

## 15. Common Mistakes

1. **Time ko ignore karna** — sirf paisa dekhna (A ki share kam hai ya zyada, time se decide hota hai).
2. **Profit ke zero bhi hata dena** (Garib banao approach me) — sirf investment ke!
3. **"After 3 more months" ko 3 months samajhna** — wo cumulative (6) hota hai.
4. **Total time 12 le lena jab question me 18 months diye ho** — dhyan se padho.
5. **Time nahi diya to ignore karna** — iska matlab poora business time.
6. **x months before me time = x lena** — time = Total − x.
7. **Add/withdraw ke baad purana amount hi use karna** — naya amount (add/minus ke baad) use karo.
8. **% increase me sirf % ko jodna** (jaise 50% increase = x + 50) — multiply (3/2) karo.

## 1. Speed = Efficiency

Time & Work me:
- **Efficiency** = work done per unit time.

Speed & Distance me:
- **Speed** = distance covered per unit time.

> **Speed hi efficiency hai** ("Shaktimaan hi Gangadhar hai"). Isliye Time & Work ke concepts yahan bhi chalte hain.

**Example:** 20 km/h ka matlab 1 ghante me 20 km, yani efficiency = 20 km per hour.

---

## 2. Basic Formulas

(Same E × T = W ka roop)

- **Distance = Speed × Time** (D = S × T)
- **Speed = Distance / Time**
- **Time = Distance / Speed**

---

## 3. Units ka Khel (Kyun D = S × T?)

**Ex:** Speed 20 km/h, time 10 hours → D = 200 km ✔
- 20 km/**h** × 10 **h** → hours cancel → bachta hai **km** = distance ✔

**Ex (galat):** Speed 40 **m/s**, time 10 **hours** → seedha 400 nahi aayega.
- Kyunki **seconds** aur **hours** cancel nahi ho rahe.
- Pehle **units same** karo.

**Rule:** Speed × Time tabhi Distance deta hai jab time ki unit speed ke denominator wali unit se cancel ho jaye.

## 4. Unit Conversion

### 4.1 km/h ↔ m/s

| Conversion | Multiply by |
|---|---|
| km/h → m/s | **5/18** |
| m/s → km/h | **18/5** |

**Yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** km/h bada hota hai, m/s chhota. Bade se chhote → chhota fraction (5/18). Chhote se bade → 18/5.

**Kyun?** 1 km/h = 1000 m / 3600 s = 5/18 m/s ✔

### 4.2 Metric ladder (distance)

**Kilo – Hecto – Deca – Meter – Deci – Centi – Milli**

- **Upar se neeche** jaana → **Multiply** (seedhi utarna)
- **Neeche se upar** jaana → **Divide** (lift se upar chadhna)
- Jitni **seedhiyan (steps)**, utne **zeros wala 1** (10, 100, 1000, ...)

**Examples:**
- 10 km → m: 3 steps neeche → ×1000 = **10,000 m**
- 15 hm → dm: 3 steps neeche → ×1000 = **15,000 dm**
- 1000 cm → hm: 4 steps upar → ÷10000 = **0.1 hm**

### 4.3 Time

**Hour – Minute – Second** (har step 60)

- hr → min: ×60
- min → sec: ×60
- Ulta: ÷60

**Ex:** 2 hr = 2 × 60 = **120 min**
**Ex:** 7200 sec → hr: 2 steps → ÷(60 × 60) = **2 hr**

## 5. Hours ko Hours + Minutes me Todna

**Ex 1:** 15/2 hr
- 15 ÷ 2 = 7 remainder 1 → 7 + 1/2 hr
- 1/2 hr = 30 min → **7 hr 30 min**

**Ex 2:** 85/4 hr
- 85 ÷ 4 = 21 remainder 1 → 21 + 1/4 hr
- 1/4 hr = 15 min → **21 hr 15 min**

**Trick:** Whole part = hours. Fraction part × 60 = minutes.

---

## 6. Speed aur Time ka Relation

**Jab distance same ho:**
- Speed badhao → time kam
- Speed ghatao → time zyada

**Inverse proportion:** S ∝ 1/T

**Ratio trick:**
- Speed ratio **a : b** → Time ratio **b : a** (palat do)

> **Condition:** Distance **same** hona chahiye.

---

## 7. Relative Speed (Raj–Simran Concept)

Jab **do speeds** aur **direction** dono aa jaye to Time = **Distance / Relative Speed**

| Case | Direction | Relative Speed | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Breakup case** | Same direction (Raj peeche, Simran aage) | **S₁ − S₂** (bada − chhota) | D / (S₁ − S₂) |
| **Patch-up case** | Opposite direction (ek doosre ki taraf) | **S₁ + S₂** | D / (S₁ + S₂) |

**Important condition:** Ye formulas tabhi lagte hain jab **dono ek hi time pe chalna shuru karein.**
Agar alag-alag time pe shuru kiya ho, to pehle ek ko pehle chala kar "time sync" karo (section 18).

## 8. Average Speed

**Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time**

### Ex1: 20 km in 3 hr, 30 km in 5 hr, 40 km in 2 hr
- Total D = 20 + 30 + 40 = 90 km
- Total T = 3 + 5 + 2 = 10 hr
- **Avg = 90/10 = 9 km/h**

### Ex2: 20 km @ 5 km/h, 40 km @ 8 km/h, 40 km @ 20 km/h
- Times: 20/5 = 4, 40/8 = 5, 40/20 = 2 → Total T = **11 hr**
- Total D = 100 km
- **Avg = 100/11 = 9.09 km/h**

**Formulas (detail):**
- Avg = (d₁ + d₂ + ...) / (t₁ + t₂ + ...)
- Jab time nahi diya ho: t = d/s se nikalo → Avg = (d₁ + d₂ + ...) / (d₁/s₁ + d₂/s₂ + ...)

---

## 9. Same Distance, Do Speeds → Average Speed

**Formula:** **Avg Speed = 2 × s₁ × s₂ / (s₁ + s₂)**

**Sirf tab jab dono speeds se tay ki gayi distance SAME ho.**

### Q: Ek aadmi ek nischit doori 20 km/h se, wahi doori 25 km/h se tay karta hai. Avg speed?
- Basic method: total D = 2d; total T = d/20 + d/25 = (5d + 4d)/100 = 9d/100 → Avg = 2d ÷ (9d/100) = **200/9 km/h**
- Formula: 2 × 20 × 25/45 = 1000/45 = **200/9 km/h** ✔

### Q: Car 80 km/h se jaati hai, 40 km/h se wapas aati hai. Avg speed?
- 2 × 80 × 40 / 120 = 6400/120 = **160/3 km/h** (60 nahi! simple average galat hai)

> **Important:** Aise questions me (80 + 40)/2 = 60 likhna **galat** hai.

### Q: Neeraj ne 40% distance 40 km/h, 50% distance 25 km/h, 10% distance 10 km/h se tay kiya. Avg?
- Total D = 100 maan lo.
- Times: 40/40 = 1, 50/25 = 2, 10/10 = 1 → Total T = 4
- **Avg = 100/4 = 25 km/h**
(Yahan distance same nahi hai isliye 2ab/(a+b) nahi, seedha total D / total T.)

## 10. Round Trip – Ghar se Office aur Wapas

**Concept:** Aap ghar se office jaate ho, wapas aate ho. **Distance same** hai, to avg speed formula lag sakta hai.

### Q: Pavita ghar se office 25 km/h se jaati hai, 10 km/h se wapas aati hai. Total time 15 hr. Ghar–office doori?

- Avg speed = 2 × 25 × 10 / 35 = **500/35 = 100/7 km/h**
- Total distance (round trip) = Avg × Time = (100/7) × 15 = 1500/7
- **Ghar se office** = half = **750/7 km**

**Rule (bahut zaroori):**
- Agar **"total distance covered"** puchha hai → full (2×) distance
- Agar **"ghar se office ki doori"** puchha hai → **half** (kyunki ek taraf ki doori)

### Q: Student school 5½ km/h se jaata hai, 4 km/h se wapas aata hai. Total time 4¾ hr. Total distance covered?
- One-way = (s₁ × s₂/(s₁ + s₂)) × T = (5.5 × 4/9.5) × 4.75 = 22/9.5 × 4.75 = **11 km**
- **Total distance covered = 22 km**

### Q: Student 5 km/h aur 4 km/h, total time 18 hr. Total distance covered?
- One-way = (5 × 4/9) × 18 = **40 km**
- **Total covered = 80 km**

**Ratio approach (alternative):** Speed 5:4 → time 4:5; total 9 units = 18 hr → 1 unit = 2 hr; jaate time 4 × 2 = 8 hr at 5 km/h → 40 km one way → **80 km** total ✔

### A → B → C (alag direction, wapas nahi)
**Q:** A → B 40 km/h, B → C 20 km/h, dono doori same, total time 120 hr. A se C doori?
- Yahan "2" **lagana padega** (kyunki dono tarf alag-alag doori tay hui, wapas nahi aaye)
- D = 2 × 40 × 20 / 60 × 120 = (1600/60) × 120 = **3200 km**

**Rule:** "2" tab nahi lagate jab wapas aa kar same doori aur nahi badhti. "2" tab rakhte hain jab total doori (jo dono hisso se banti hai) chahiye.

## 11. Late / Early Wale Questions

### Time Difference ka rule (bahut important)
- **Late + Early** (ek late, ek early) → time diff = **ADD**
- **Late + Late** → **SUBTRACT**
- **Early + Early** → **SUBTRACT**

### Formula (same distance, do speeds, time diff diya ho)

**D = (S₁ × S₂ / (S₁ − S₂)) × (Time Difference)**

(S₂ > S₁, time diff ghanto me)

### Q1: Rahul 40 km/h se college 25 min late pahunchta hai. Agle din speed 10 km/h badhata hai (50), phir bhi 10 min late. College kitni door?
- Dono late → time diff = 25 − 10 = **15 min = 1/4 hr**
- D = (40 × 50 / 10) × (1/4) = 200 × 1/4 = **50 km**

### Q2: Bus ki speed 30% badhane par 2 hr jaldi pahunchti hai. Usual time?
- 30% = 3/10 → speed 10 : 13
- Time ratio = 13 : 10 (palat)
- 3 units = 2 hr → 1 unit = 2/3
- **Usual time (13 units) = 13 × 2/3 = 26/3 hr**
- New time = 10 × 2/3 = 20/3 hr

### Q3: Amit, Babita se 2 hr 30 min zyada leta hai 40 km me. Agar Amit speed double kare to Babita se 1 hr kam lagega. Amit ko kitna time lagta hai?
- Pehle **late** 2.5 hr, baad me **early** 1 hr → Time diff = 2.5 + 1 = **3.5 hr**
- Speed double (1 : 2) → time 2 : 1 → difference = 1 ratio unit
- 1 unit = 3.5 hr = 7/2 hr
- Original time = 2 units = 2 × 7/2 = **7 hr**

**Note:** "If" wali condition hypothetical hai, ye ORIGINAL nahi hai. Puchha ORIGINAL time hai isliye "2 units" (bada wala) lo.

## 12. Variable Speed Wale Questions (Mains level)

### Q1: Bus ki speed 6 km/h zyada hone par 6 hr kam, 6 km/h kam hone par 10 hr zyada. Distance?
Maan lo original speed = s.
- Case 1: D = (s × (s + 6)/6) × 6
- Case 2: D = (s × (s − 6)/6) × 10
- Dono equal: s(s+6) = s(s−6) × 10/6
- (s + 6) × 3 = 5(s − 6)... (s cancel, 6 cancel, 2 se cancel) → 3(s + 6) = 5(s − 6)
- 3s + 18 = 5s − 30 → 2s = 48 → **s = 24 km/h**
- D = (24 × 30/6) × 6 = **720 km** ✔ (check: (24 × 18/6) × 10 = 72 × 10 = 720 ✔)

### Q2: x km @ 40 km/h me (x + 20) km @ 60 km/h se 2 hr zyada. x + 40 km @ 40 km/h me kitna time?
- x/40 − (x + 20)/60 = 2
- LCM 120: 3x − 2(x + 20) = 240 → x − 40 = 240 → **x = 280**
- x + 40 = 320 km, time = 320/40 = **8 hr**

### Q3: P → Q 90 km/h, Q → R 60 km/h, P se R = 200 km, avg speed 75. P–Q doori?
- x + y = 200 ... (1)
- Avg = total D / total T → 75 = 200 / (x/90 + y/60)
- x/90 + y/60 = 200/75 = 8/3
- LCM 180: (2x + 3y)/180 = 8/3 → 2x + 3y = 480 ... (2)
- (2) − 2 × (1): y = 480 − 400 = **80**
- x = 200 − 80 = **120 km**

**Lesson:** Variable aaye to **do equations** banao (x + y = total, aur avg speed/time wali equation) aur solve karo.

## 13. Stoppage

**Formula:**

**Stoppage per hour (min) = [(Speed without stoppage − Speed with stoppage) / Speed without stoppage] × 60**

### Q: Bus ki speed bina stoppage ke 54 km/h, stoppage ke saath 45 km/h. Per hour kitne min rukti hai?
- Difference = 54 − 45 = 9
- 9/54 × 60 = **10 minutes**

**Logic:** Bina ruke 54 km chalti, ruk-ruk ke 45 km. 9 km kam gayi. 9 km 54 km/h se tay karne me 9/54 hr = 10 min lagte → utni der rukti hai.

## 14. Special Case 1: Do Log Ek Doosre Ki Taraf, Meeting ke baad Time Diye ho

**Situation:** A aur B ek hi time par (jaise 9 AM) ek doosre ki taraf chalte hain. Milne ke **baad**:
- A ko apni manzil tak pahunchne me **t₁** time lagta hai
- B ko apni manzil tak pahunchne me **t₂** time lagta hai

**Meeting time (start se) = √(t₁ × t₂)**

### Q: Abhay A se B (9 AM), Rekha B se A (9 AM). Meeting ke baad dono ko manzil tak 24 min aur 54 min lagte hain. Meeting kitne baje?
- Meeting time = √(24 × 54) = √1296 = **36 min**
- Meeting at **9:36 AM**

---

## 15. Special Case 2: Meeting ke baad Speed Ratio

**Wahi situation:** A aur B ek doosre ki taraf. Meeting ke baad A ko t₁ aur B ko t₂ lagta hai.

**Formula:** **S_A / S_B = √(t₂ / t₁)**

(t₁ = A ka meeting ke baad time, t₂ = B ka meeting ke baad time)

### Q: A aur B ek doosre se milne ke baad apni-apni destination tak pahunchte hain. A ko 6⅛ hr (=49/8), B ko 8 hr lagte hain. A ki speed 32 km/h. B ki speed?
- S_A/S_B = √(t_B / t_A) = √(8 ÷ (49/8)) = √(64/49) = **8/7**
- 32 / S_B = 8/7 → **S_B = 28 km/h**

*(Is formula me direct values daalo, derive karne ki zarurat nahi.)*

## 16. Do Vehicles, Same Distance, Alag Time

### Q: Bus A 40 km/h, Bus B 55 km/h, same jagah se start, B 2 hr jaldi pahunchti hai. Distance?
- Maan lo B ko t hr lagte hain → A ko (t + 2)
- Dono ne same distance tay ki: 40(t + 2) = 55t
- 40t + 80 = 55t → 15t = 80 → **t = 16/3 hr**
- D = 55 × 16/3 = **880/3 km**

**Shortcut formula:** D = (S₁S₂/(S₂ − S₁)) × Δt = (40 × 55/15) × 2 = 2200/15 × 2 = 880/3 ✔

## 17. Walk–Ride Type

### Q: Main ek taraf chal ke aur ek taraf bike se 45 min me jaata hoon. Dono taraf chal ke 55 min lagte hain. Dono taraf bike se kitna time?
- Maan lo ek taraf chalne ka time = w, bike = r
- w + r = 45
- 2w = 55 → w = 27.5
- r = 45 − 27.5 = 17.5
- Dono taraf bike = 2r = **35 min**

## 18. Alag-alag Time Pe Start (Head Start)

**Rule:** Relative speed formula tabhi lagao jab dono **same time pe chal rahe ho**. Isliye jo pehle chala hai use pehle **akele chala do** jab tak doosra start na kare. Phir bachi hui distance par relative speed lagao.

### Q: A 60 km/h se 8 AM par P se, B 70 km/h se 8:30 AM par Q se, PQ = 680 km. Kab milenge?
- 8:00–8:30: A akele 30 min → 30 km tay kiya
- Bacha = 680 − 30 = **650 km**
- Ab dono chal rahe, ek doosre ki taraf → S = 60 + 70 = 130
- Time = 650/130 = **5 hr**
- Meeting time = 8:30 + 5 hr = **1:30 PM**

## 19. Speed Change Beech Me

### Q: PQ = 440 km. Car A, P se 9 AM pe 55 km/h. Car B, Q se 11 AM pe 45 km/h. Car A 12 noon par speed 15 km/h badha deta hai. Kab milenge?
- 9 AM → 12 PM (3 hr): A ne 55 × 3 = **165 km**
- 11 AM → 12 PM (1 hr): B ne 45 × 1 = **45 km**
- 12 baje dono ki ghadi same, bacha = 440 − (165 + 45) = **230 km**
- Ab A ki naya speed = 55 + 15 = 70; B = 45
- Opposite direction: 70 + 45 = 115
- Time = 230/115 = **2 hr**
- Meeting time = 12 PM + 2 hr = **2 PM**

## 20. Train Type (Time diya, Speed aur Distance nahi)

### Q: Ek train A se 7 AM pe chalkar B pe 11 AM pe pahunchti hai. Doosri train B se 8 AM pe chalkar A pe 11:30 AM pe pahunchti hai. Dono kab milengi?

**Step 1:** Times nikalo
- Train 1 = 7 AM → 11 AM = **4 hr**
- Train 2 = 8 AM → 11:30 AM = **3.5 hr = 7/2 hr**

**Step 2 (Time & Work wala trick):** LCM(4, 7/2) → numerators 4/1 aur 7/2 → LCM = **28** (Total Distance)
- Speed 1 = 28/4 = **7**
- Speed 2 = 28/(7/2) = **8**

**Step 3:** Train 1 ne 7–8 AM akele **7 km** tay kiye → Bacha = 28 − 7 = **21**

**Step 4:** Ab dono chal rahe (8 AM se), S = 7 + 8 = 15
- Time = 21/15 = **7/5 hr = 1 hr 24 min**
- Meeting = 8:00 + 1:24 = **9:24 AM**

## 21. Thief–Police (Chor–Police)

### Q: Police ko 300 m door chor dikhta hai. Chor 5 m/s, Police 7 m/s. Kitne time me pakdega? Chor aur police ne kitni doori tay ki?

- Same direction → relative speed = 7 − 5 = **2 m/s**
- Time = 300/2 = **150 sec**
- **Chor ne tay ki** = 5 × 150 = **750 m**
- **Police ne tay ki** = 7 × 150 = **1050 m**
(Check: 1050 − 750 = 300 ✔)

## 23. Common Mistakes

1. **Units mix karna** (m/s × hours).
2. **Simple average speed** (80 + 40)/2 = 60 likh dena — galat; 2ab/(a + b) lagao.
3. **Round trip me "2" lagana ya bhoolna** — "total distance covered" vs "ghar se office ki doori" me farq dhyan se dekho.
4. **Late + early ko subtract karna** — add karna hota hai.
5. **Alag-alag start time pe seedha relative speed lagana** — pehle time sync karo.
6. **Special case formulas me t₁, t₂ ka order ulta kar dena** — S_A/S_B = √(t_B/t_A).
7. **"If" wali hypothetical condition ko original maan lena** (Amit wale question me).
8. **Same distance condition check na karna** — 2ab/(a + b) tabhi lagta hai jab dono speed par distance barabar ho.

## Study Tips (Sir ki advice)

- Pehle basic units aur conversion pakke karo.
- Har formula ke saath uski **condition** bhi note karo (same time start, same distance).
- Teesra step hamesha: **bacha hua distance + relative speed** (head start ke case me).
- Mains level ke liye **variable (x, y) se equations** banana seekho.

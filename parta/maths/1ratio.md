## 1. Ratio kya hota hai?

- Ratio ka matlab = **Comparison** (tulna).
- Jab bhi hum 2 cheezon ko compare karte hain, hum ratio use karte hain.
- Hindi mein: **Anupaat**.

**Example (kyun invent hua):**
Ram ke paas ₹100 hai, Shyam ke paas ₹200. Dono apni exact income batana nahi chahte, lekin compare karna chahte hain.

- "Shyam ke paas Ram se **double** hai" → Shyam : Ram = **2 : 1**
- "Ram ke paas Shyam ka **aadha** hai" → Ram : Shyam = **1 : 2**

Exact value chhupi rahi, comparison bhi ho gaya. Yahi ratio ka kaam hai.

---

## 2. Ratio = Fraction (dono same cheez hain)

- Fraction bhi compare karne ke liye use hota hai, ratio bhi.
- **Rule:** Jis se compare kar rahe ho (jiske *saath*), wo **neeche (denominator)**. Jiski baat ho rahi hai, wo **upar (numerator)**.

| Statement | Fraction | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Ram ki Shyam se tulna (Ram : Shyam) | 1/2 | 1 : 2 |
| Shyam ki Ram se tulna (Shyam : Ram) | 2/1 | 2 : 1 |

**Yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** "Kiski baat? → upar. Kisse compare? → neeche."

## 3. Ratio Simplify karna

**Trick:** Dono numbers ko ek **common number** se divide karo jab tak aur divide na ho sake (HCF se ek baar mein bhi ho jaata hai).

| Given | Common divisor | Simplified |
|---|---|---|
| 45 : 95 | 5 | **9 : 19** |
| 125 : 135 | 5 | **25 : 27** |
| 64 : 124 | 2, phir 2 | 32:62 → **16 : 31** |
| 80 : 128 | 4, 2, 2 (ya seedha 16) | **5 : 8** |

**Tip:** Agar bada number ek baar mein samajh na aaye, to chhote-chhote tukdon mein (2, 4 se) divide karo. Kuch galat nahi hai.

**Likhne/bolne ka tareeka:** 16 : 31 ko bolte hain "16 is to 31" ya Hindi mein "16 anupaat 31".

## 4. Golden Rule: UNITS SAME honi chahiye

- Ratio hamesha **same units** ke beech hota hai.
- Length ki length se, area ki area se, colour ki colour se, rupaye ki rupaye se compare hoti hai.

**Example:** ₹ aur km ka ratio **possible hi nahi** hai. Aisa question aaye to answer: **"Cannot be determined / not possible"** (video mein ise option C & D / "CND" bola gaya tha).

Trap: Kuch students ₹100 : 600 km ka ratio 1:6 bana dete hain. **Galat!**

## 5. Proportion kya hota hai?

- Hindi: **Samaan anupaat**.
- Jab **do ratios aapas mein equal** ho, to unhe **proportion** kehte hain.

**Kaise check karein:** Dono ratios ko simplify karo. Agar simplified value same aaye → **proportion me hain**. Warna → **not in proportion**.

**Examples:**

- 2 : 3 aur 4 : 6 → 4:6 ko 2 se divide = 2:3 → **Proportion ✔**
- 40 : 50 aur 80 : 100 → pehla = 4:5, doosra = 4:5 → **Proportion ✔**
  (Likhte hain: "40, 50, 80 and 100 are in proportion")

**Note:** Ye basic lagta hai, lekin Mains mein "values proportional hain" jaise words aate hain. Isliye term clear honi chahiye.

## 6. Ratio se Real Value – "x" kyun lagate hain?

**Observation:** Alag-alag real values ka ratio same ho sakta hai.

| Real values | Simplified ratio | Multiplier (2:3 se real tak) |
|---|---|---|
| 20 : 30 | 2 : 3 | × 10 |
| 40 : 60 | 2 : 3 | × 20 |
| 100 : 150 | 2 : 3 | × 50 |
| 30 : 45 | 2 : 3 | × 15 |

**Key lessons:**

1. Ek hi ratio ke peeche **anginat (infinite)** real values ho sakti hain.
2. Ratio se real value tak jaane ke liye **ek unique number** se **dono ko multiply** karna padta hai (wahi same number dono par).
3. Agar real value pata nahi hai, to us unique number ko **x** maan lo.

**Isiliye:** Ratio 4 : 5 ho to hum likhte hain **4x aur 5x**. Ratio par directly calculation nahi ho sakti, pehle usse real value (x ke through) me convert karna padta hai.

**Imp:** Ek ratio ka "1 unit" sirf usi ratio set ke liye valid hai. Alag ratio set ke liye alag unit hoga (aage question me dikhega).

## 7. Subtraction of Ratios (Internal Gap Concept)

Do ratios ko aapas me **subtract** kar sakte hain, **sirf tab jab dono ka internal gap SAME ho**.

**Internal gap = ratio ke dono parts ka difference.**

### Examples (gap already same)

- 4 : 9 aur 9 : 14 → gap 5 aur 5 → subtract: **5 : 5** ✔
- 2 : 3 aur 4 : 5 → gap 1 aur 1 → subtract karke (4−2) : (5−3) = **2 : 2** ✔

### Wrong example (jo galat hai, note karo)

- 3 : 4 aur 7 : 9 → gap 1 aur 2 (**same nahi**) → seedha subtract karna **GALAT** hai. Pehle gap same karna padega.

### Gap same kaise karein (Trick)

**Step 1:** Dono ratios ka gap nikalo.
**Step 2:** Kisi ratio ko ek number se multiply karke gap barabar karo.
**Step 3:** Ab subtract karo.

**Example A:** 3 : 5 aur 7 : 11
- Gap: 2 aur 4
- Pehle ratio ko 2 se multiply: 6 : 10 (gap 4)
- Ab subtract: (7−6) : (11−10) = **1 : 1**

**Example B:** 5 : 11 aur 4 : 7
- Gap: 6 aur 3
- Doosre ko 2 se multiply: 8 : 14 (gap 6)
- Subtract: (8−5) : (14−11) = **3 : 3**

**Example C (cross-multiply trick):** 7 : 9 aur 11 : 14
- Gap: 2 aur 3
- Pehle ko **3** se (gap 6), doosre ko **2** se (gap 6) multiply karo → 21 : 27 aur 22 : 28
- Subtract: **1 : 1**

**Rule of thumb:** Gap alag-alag ho to **cross multiply** karke gap same banao.

## 8. Distribution of Money in a Ratio

**Question:** ₹140 ko Rahul aur Aul me 2 : 3 ke ratio me baantna hai.

### Method 1 (Unit method – sabse safe)

1. Total ratio units = 2 + 3 = **5**
2. 5 units = ₹140 → **1 unit = 140 ÷ 5 = ₹28**
3. Rahul = 2 × 28 = **₹56**
4. Aul = 3 × 28 = **₹84**

### Method 2 (Shortcut)

**Share = Total amount × (person ka ratio part) ÷ (total ratio)**

- Rahul = 140 × 2 / 5 = **56**
- Aul = 140 × 3 / 5 = **84**

**Yaad rakho:** "Jiska hissa chahiye, uska ratio multiply, total se divide."

**Golden line:** Agar **1 unit** ki value mil gayi, to poora brahmaand tumhare haath me hai.

## 9. Combining Ratios: A:B aur B:C se A:B:C

### 9.1 Bahurupiya Approach (Universal – kabhi fail nahi hota)

**Idea:** Jo letter **do jagah** hai lekin **alag numbers** ke saath (bahurupiya = disguise me), use pakdo aur uske numbers **same** karo (dono ratios ko multiply karke).

**Example 1:** A : B = 2 : 3 aur B : C = 6 : 7
- B bahurupiya hai (3 aur 6).
- Pehle ratio ko 2 se multiply: A : B = 4 : 6
- Ab B same hai (6) → **A : B : C = 4 : 6 : 7**

**Example 2:** A : B = 5 : 14 aur B : C = 7 : 9
- B: 14 aur 7. Doosre ratio ko 2 se multiply: B : C = 14 : 18
- **A : B : C = 5 : 14 : 18**

**Example 3 (bahurupiya A ho sakta hai):** A : B = 2 : 3 aur A : C = 5 : 6
- A: 2 aur 5 → LCM 10
- Pehle ko 5 se multiply: A : B = 10 : 15
- Doosre ko 2 se multiply: A : C = 10 : 12
- **A : B : C = 10 : 15 : 12**

> Bahurupiya sirf B hi nahi, kabhi bhi A ya C ho sakta hai.

### 9.2 "Ulta-N" Approach (Cross multiplication – sirf shortcut)

**Kab lagta hai:** Jab ek ratio ka **end** doosre ratio ka **start** ho (A:B, **B**:C). Yani chain continuous ho.

**Example:** A : B = 5 : 14 aur B : C = 7 : 9
- A = 5 × 7 = 35
- B = 14 × 7 = 98
- C = 14 × 9 = 126
- Ab 7 se divide: **5 : 14 : 18** ✔

**Kab FAIL hota hai:** Jab chain continuous nahi hai, jaise A:B aur **A**:C (A dono me repeat). Example 3 me Ulta-N lagane se galat answer aata hai.

**Lesson:** Pata hona chahiye kaun si approach kahan lagti hai. Agar confusion ho to **Bahurupiya** use karo (ye hamesha kaam karta hai).

## 10. Trick: 2A = 3B (aur 2A = 3B = 4C)

### 10.1 Do variables: 2A = 3B

**Shortcut ("padosan"/side-by-side approach):**
- A ke saamne **B ka number**, B ke saamne **A ka number**.
- 2A = 3B → **A : B = 3 : 2**

### 10.2 Teen variables: 2A = 3B = 4C

**Method 1 – Latkan (Hanging) Approach:**
1. Letters ke saamne wale numbers (2, 3, 4) ka **LCM** lo = 12.
2. 12 ko har number se divide karo:
   - A = 12 ÷ 2 = **6**
   - B = 12 ÷ 3 = **4**
   - C = 12 ÷ 4 = **3**
3. **A : B : C = 6 : 4 : 3**

**Method 2 – Hide and Seek (Lukachuppi) Approach:**
- Jis letter ki value chahiye use **chhupa do**, baaki bache numbers ko **multiply** karo.
- A chahiye → B aur C ke numbers multiply: 3 × 4 = **12**
- B chahiye → A aur C: 2 × 4 = **8**
- C chahiye → A aur B: 2 × 3 = **6**
- **12 : 8 : 6** → simplify (÷2) → **6 : 4 : 3** ✔

**Another example (practice):** 3A = 4B = 6C
- Hide & Seek: A = 4×6 = 24, B = 3×6 = 18, C = 3×4 = 12 → 24:18:12 → **4 : 3 : 2**

**Example (video):** 5A = 3B = 4C (type ka question) bhi isi tarah hoga: A = 3×4 = 12, B = 5×4 = 20, C = 5×3 = 15 → **12 : 20 : 15**.

## 11. Fractions wale ratio ko Whole Numbers me convert karna

**Rule:** Ratios hamesha **whole numbers** me hote hain, fractions me nahi.

**Steps:**
1. Mixed fraction ko improper banao.
2. Sab **denominators ka LCM** lo.
3. Us LCM se **sabko multiply** karo.

**Example:** 1/2 : 16 2/3 : 1/4
- 16 2/3 = (16×3 + 2)/3 = **50/3**
- Denominators: 2, 3, 4 → LCM = **12**
- Multiply: (1/2)×12 = **6**, (50/3)×12 = **200**, (1/4)×12 = **3**
- **Ratio = 6 : 200 : 3**

## 12. Chaar (4) Quantities: A : B : C : D

Given: A : B, B : C, C : D → A : B : C : D nikalna hai.

### 12.1 Zameen Kabza Approach (Land-grabbing)

**Idea:** Khaali jagah ko **padosi number** bhar deta hai. Phir **vertical multiply** karo.

**Example:** A : B = 2 : 3, B : C = 4 : 5, C : D = 1 : 2

Table:

| | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A:B | 2 | 3 | – | – |
| B:C | – | 4 | 5 | – |
| C:D | – | – | 1 | 2 |

Khaali jagah padosi se bharo (left se padosi ka number, right se padosi ka):

- A column: 2, 4, 1 ... video me method: har column ke number multiply karne se:
  - A = 2 × 4 × 1 = **8**
  - B = 3 × 4 × 1 = **12**
  - C = 3 × 5 × 1 = **15**
  - D = 3 × 5 × 2 = **30**
- **A : B : C : D = 8 : 12 : 15 : 30**

### 12.2 Sandwich Approach (same question, naya tareeka)

**Example:** A : B = 1 : 5, B : C = 2 : 3, C : D = 4 : 7

**Bread (pehle vertical multiply, A–B–C ke "start" wale numbers):**
- A = 1 × 2 × 4 = **8**
- D = 5 × 3 × 7 = **105**

**Aloo (filling – beech wale numbers):**
- B = 5 × 2 × 4 = **40**
- C = 5 × 3 × 4 = **60**

**Final: A : B : C : D = 8 : 40 : 60 : 105** (Zameen Kabza se bhi yahi aata hai.)

Shortcut sochne ka tareeka: **Aage wale numbers ka multiplication aur peeche wale numbers ka multiplication** milakar dono ko beech ke numbers se adjust karte jao.

### 12.3 Mahabharat Approach (sirf A : D chahiye ho)

**Idea:** Kaurav (denominator wale numbers) aur Pandav (numerator wale numbers) ki do team. Aamne-saamne wale **same players ko kaat do**.

**Formula:**

**A : D = (A-parts ka product) : (B-parts ka product)**

Jaise A:B = a1:b1, B:C = a2:b2, C:D = a3:b3 to

**A : D = (a1 × a2 × a3) : (b1 × b2 × b3)**

**Example:** A : B = 2 : 3, B : C = 7 : 2, C : D = 6 : 1
- A : D = (2 × 7 × 6) : (3 × 2 × 1) = 84 : 6 = **14 : 1**
- Pehle hi cancel kar do: 2 aur 2 cancel, 6 aur 3 cancel → quick answer.

> Jab sirf pehla aur aakhri part poochha ho (A : D), tab Mahabharat approach time bachati hai.

## 13. Coins Wale Questions

### 13.1 Basic concepts (bahut zaroori)

**Concept 1: Chhote se bade me jaana = DIVIDE, bade se chhote me aana = MULTIPLY.**
(Yaad rakhne ke liye: Ground floor se upar jaane ke liye **lift** use karte ho → lift ka sign ÷ jaisa dikhta hai.)

**Concept 2: Fraction value of paise (₹1 se kam ke coins):**

Fraction value = **paise / 100**

| Paise | Fraction value of ₹1 |
|---|---|
| 25 p | 1/4 |
| 20 p | 1/5 |
| 10 p | 1/10 |
| 5 p | 1/20 |
| 50 p | 1/2 |
| 75 p | 3/4 |

(Fraction value tabhi chahiye jab coin ₹1 se chhota ho. ₹1, ₹2, ₹5 jaise coins ki value waise hi lete hain.)

**Concept 3: "Jiski lathi, uski bhains" Approach**

- Agar **total Rupees (amount)** diya hai → ratio bhi **Rupees ka** chahiye.
- Agar **total coins ki sankhya** di hai → ratio bhi **coins ka** chahiye.
- Dono mismatch ho to pehle convert karo (coins ke ratio ko rupees ke ratio me, ya ulta).

### 13.2 Solved Example 1

**Q:** Ek bag me 50 paise, 25 paise aur ₹1 ke coins 5 : 8 : 1 ke ratio me hain. Total value ₹55 hai. 25 paise ke kitne coins hain?

- Total **rupees** diya hai (₹55), ratio **coins** ka hai → mismatch. Coins ratio ko rupees ratio me badlo.
- Rupees ratio:
  - 50p: 5 × (1/2) = **2.5**
  - 25p: 8 × (1/4) = **2**
  - ₹1: 1 × 1 = **1**
- Total = 2.5 + 2 + 1 = **5.5 units** = ₹55
- 1 unit = 55 ÷ 5.5 = **10**
- 25p ke coins = **8 coins-ratio** × 10 = **80 coins** ✔

### 13.3 Solved Example 2

**Q:** Ek bag me ₹5, ₹10 aur ₹20 ke coins 2 : 3 : 5 (coins ke ratio) me hain. Total coins = 240. Total amount kitna hai?

- Total **coins** diya (240), ratio bhi **coins** ka → koi convert nahi.
- Total ratio = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 → 1 unit = 240 ÷ 10 = **24 coins**
- ₹5 ke coins = 2 × 24 = **48** → 48 × 5 = **₹240**
- ₹10 ke coins = 3 × 24 = **72** → 72 × 10 = **₹720**
- ₹20 ke coins = 5 × 24 = **120** → 120 × 20 = **₹2400**
- **Total amount = 240 + 720 + 2400 = ₹3360** ✔

**Lesson:** Teen steps hamesha yaad rakho: (1) kya total diya hai, (2) ratio kis cheez ka hai, (3) mismatch ho to convert karo.

## 14. Same Number Add / Subtract (Increase-Decrease Questions)

**Core concept:** Agar dono quantities me **same number add** karne se ratio ban raha hai, to **Old ratio aur New ratio ko subtract** karne par jo gap aata hai wo added number ke barabar hota hai.

### Q1: Do numbers 3 : 4 me hain. Dono me 30 add karne par ratio 9 : 10 ho jaata hai. Original numbers?

- Internal gap: 3:4 ka gap 1, 9:10 ka gap 1 (same ✔)
- Subtract: (9−3) : (10−4) = **6 : 6**
- 6 units = 30 → **1 unit = 5**
- Original numbers = 3×5 = **15** aur 4×5 = **20**

### Q2: Teen classes ka ratio 3 : 4 : 5. Har class me 20 students badhne par ratio 4 : 5 : 6 ho jaata hai. Original total students?

- Gap (new − old) = 1 unit har class me → 1 unit = 20
- Original total = (3+4+5) = 12 units = 12 × 20 = **240**

### Q3 (important – alag-alag number add ho rahe hain): 3 : 5 : 8 ratio me students. Teen sections me 15, 30, 15 aur students aaye, naya ratio 4 : 7 : 9. Original total?

- Increase ratio units: 4−3 = **1** unit = 15, 7−5 = **2** units = 30, 9−8 = **1** unit = 15
- Har jagah **1 unit = 15** (consistent ✔)
- Original total = (3+5+8) = 16 units = 16 × 15 = **240**

**Note:** Yahan gap same nahi tha (2, 3), lekin humne gap ki jagah **increase ko ratio units me dekha** aur check kiya ki "1 unit" teeno me same hai. Ye isliye kaam kiya kyunki increase same unit-rate se ho raha tha.

## 15. Income – Expenditure – Savings Questions

**Basic formulas (yaad rakho):**

- **Savings = Income − Expenditure**
- **Income = Expenditure + Savings**
- **Expenditure = Income − Savings**

### Q1 (Same savings): A aur B ki income ka ratio 11 : 13, expenditure ka ratio 9 : 11. Dono ki savings ₹4000 hain. Dono ki income me difference?

**Method 1 – Subtract approach (sirf jab savings SAME ho):**
- Income ratio − Expenditure ratio:
  - A: 11 − 9 = **2 units**
  - B: 13 − 11 = **2 units** (savings same ✔)
- Ye 2 units = ₹4000 → **1 unit = ₹2000**
- Income difference = 13 − 11 = **2 units = ₹4000**

**Method 2 – "Panda Approach" (Universal – savings same ho ya na ho):**

Idea: Ek variable (expenditure) ko **eliminate** karne ke liye cross multiply karo.

- Income: A = 11x, B = 13x; Expenditure: A = 9y, B = 11y
- Equation 1: 11x − 9y = 4000; Equation 2: 13x − 11y = 4000
- Trick: **Income ratio × other's expenditure ratio** (cross):
  - 11 × 11 = 121 aur 9 × 13 = 117
  - 11 × 4000 = 44000 aur 9 × 4000 = 36000
- Subtract: 121 − 117 = **4 units**; 44000 − 36000 = **8000**
- 4 units = 8000 → **1 unit = 2000**
- Income difference = 2 units = **₹4000** ✔ (dono methods ka same answer)
- Agar individual income chahiye: A = 11 × 2000 = **₹22,000**, B = 13 × 2000 = **₹26,000**

> **Panda Approach universal hai**: savings alag ho to bhi chalegi.

### Q2 (Mains level): A aur B ki income ka ratio 5 : 3. A, B aur C ke expenditure ka ratio 8 : 5 : 2. C ₹2000 kharch karta hai aur B ₹700 bachata hai. A kitna bachata hai?

- C ka expenditure: 2 units = ₹2000 → **1 unit (expenditure) = ₹1000**
- B ka expenditure = 5 × 1000 = **₹5000**
- B ki income = 5000 + 700 = **₹5700**
- B ka income ratio = 3 units = 5700 → **1 unit (income) = ₹1900**
- A ki income = 5 × 1900 = **₹9500**
- A ka expenditure = 8 × 1000 = **₹8000**
- **A ki savings = 9500 − 8000 = ₹1500** ✔

**Warning:** Expenditure ka 1 unit (₹1000) aur income ka 1 unit (₹1900) **alag-alag** hain. Dono ko mix mat karo. Har ratio ka apna unit hota hai.

## 16. "Increase in a Ratio" – Sahara (Support) Concept

**Problem type:** Ek value ko ek ratio ke through doosre ratio me pahunchana.

**Concept:** 40 ko 2 : 3 ke ratio me increase karo.

- Old value ko **2** ka sahara milta hai, destination **3** hai.
- 2 units = 40 → 1 unit = 20
- 3 units = 3 × 20 = **60**

**Formula/Trick:** **(Value ÷ old ratio part) × new ratio part.**

(Jaise teacher "sahara" dekar student ko manzil tak pahunchata hai.)

### Solved Example (Premium question)

**Q:** A, B, C ki last year ki income 3 : 4 : 5 ke ratio me thi. Last year se current year income ke ratio A: 4 : 5, B: 2 : 3, C: 3 : 4. Agar total current year income ₹98,500 hai, to B aur C ki combined current income?

**Step 1 – Current year me convert karo (sahara):**
- A: 3 × (5/4) = **15/4**
- B: 4 × (3/2) = **6**
- C: 5 × (4/3) = **20/3**

**Step 2 – Whole numbers me lao (LCM 12):**
- A = 15/4 × 12 = **45**, B = 6 × 12 = **72**, C = 20/3 × 12 = **80**
- Total = 45 + 72 + 80 = **197 units** (12 se divide karne ka mauka aata hai, yani total = 197/12)

**Step 3 – 1 unit nikalo:**
- Total current income = ₹98,500 = 197/12 units
- 1 unit = 98500 × 12 ÷ 197 = **₹6000** (197 × 500 = 98500, so 500 × 12 = 6000)

**Step 4 – B + C:**
- B + C = 6 + 20/3 = **38/3 units**
- = 38/3 × 6000 = 38 × 2000 = **₹76,000** ✔

**Note:** Ye question tough nahi hai. Fractions ki wajah se daravna lagta hai. Agar numbers whole hote to bahut easy lagta.

## 17. Age Problems

**Concept:** Age ka difference **kabhi nahi badalta**. Time ka gap alag-alag ratio me alag units ke barabar hota hai.

### Question

Mother aur daughter ki present ages ka ratio **7 : 1**. **5 saal baad** ratio **4 : 1** ho jaayega. Unki present ages ka difference?

**Step 1 – Internal gap same karo:**
- Present ratio 7 : 1 → gap = 6
- Future ratio 4 : 1 → gap = 3
- Future ratio ko 2 se multiply: **8 : 2** → gap = 6 ✔

**Step 2 – Subtract (future − present):**
- (8 − 7) : (2 − 1) = **1 : 1**
- 1 unit = **5 years** (kyunki 5 saal baad ka difference)

**Step 3 – Present ages:**
- Mother = 7 × 5 = **35 years**
- Daughter = 1 × 5 = **5 years**
- **Difference = 35 − 5 = 30 years** ✔

## 18. Practice Questions (Solved)

### P1: Teen numbers 1/2 : 2/3 : 3/4 ke ratio me hain. Sabse bade aur sabse chhote ka difference 27 hai. Sabse chhota number?

- LCM(2, 3, 4) = 12 → ratio = **6 : 8 : 9**
- Difference = 9 − 6 = **3 units = 27** → 1 unit = 9
- Sabse chhota = 6 × 9 = **54** ✔

### P2 (Mini concept): Agar 15 units = 45 hain, to 108 units = ?

- 1 unit = 45 ÷ 15 = **3**
- 108 units = 108 × 3 = **324** ✔

### P3: ₹10,500 ko A, B, C me 5 : 7 : 9 me baanta gaya. Har ek ko ₹500 aur mile. Naya ratio?

- **Smart way:** Dono me same ₹500 add ho raha hai, isliye ratio units ke liye 500 = 1 unit hi hai (total 21 units = 10500 → 1 unit = 500). Add 1 unit har ek me:
- 6 : 8 : 10 → simplify (÷2) → **3 : 4 : 5** ✔

(Long way: 2500, 3500, 4500 → +500 → 3000 : 4000 : 5000 = 3 : 4 : 5. Dono same.)

### P4: Galti se ₹702 ko 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/6 ki jagah 3 : 4 : 6 me baanta gaya. Ram, Ramesh, Naresh me sabse zyada fayda kise hua aur kitna?

- Correct ratio: LCM 12 → 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/6 = 4 : 3 : 2
- Galat ratio: 3 : 4 : 6
- Naresh ko sahi me 2 part milne the, galat me 6 mile → **Naresh ko sabse zyada fayda**.
- Sahi share: 702 × 2/9 = **₹156**
- Galat share: 702 × 6/13 = **₹324**
- **Fayda = 324 − 156 = ₹168** ✔

## 20. Common Mistakes (Note karke rakho!)

1. **Units alag hain (₹ aur km) aur ratio bana diya** → Galat. Answer "cannot be determined".
2. **Internal gap same kiye bina ratios subtract kar diye** → Galat. Pehle gap same karo.
3. **Ulta-N approach har jagah laga diya** → Sirf chain ratios (A:B, B:C) me lagta hai, A:B aur A:C me nahi.
4. **Alag-alag ratio set ka "1 unit" mix kar diya** (jaise expenditure aur income ke unit) → Har ratio ka apna unit.
5. **Coins me total (₹ ya coins) aur ratio ka type mismatch ignore kiya** → pehle same type me convert karo.
6. **Ratio me fractions chhod diye** → Ratio hamesha whole numbers me hota hai, LCM se multiply karo.
7. **Questions ko jaldi me padhna** ("Total cost of coins ₹55" ko "55 coins" samajh lena) → words dhyan se padho.

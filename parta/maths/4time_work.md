# Time and Work – Complete Notes

## 1. What Is Time and Work?

Time and Work is a mathematics topic that deals with:
- Total work
- Time taken to complete work
- Efficiency of a person
- People working together
- People joining or leaving a job
- Work completed in parts
- Men, women, children, and working hours

The main concept is:

**Work = Efficiency × Time**

For example, if a person makes 20 toys in one day, then in 10 days:

Total Work = 20 × 10 = 200 toys

Here:
- Efficiency = 20 toys per day
- Time = 10 days
- Total Work = 200 toys

---

## 2. Important Terms

### A. Total Work

The complete amount of work that needs to be done.

Examples:
- Making 100 toys
- Completing a project
- Building a wall

### B. Time

The time required to complete the work.

It may be measured in:
- Seconds
- Minutes
- Hours
- Days
- Months

### C. Efficiency

Efficiency means the amount of work completed in one unit of time.

Formula:

Efficiency = Total Work / Time

For example, if a person completes 100 units of work in 10 days:

Efficiency = 100 / 10 = 10 units per day

**Remember:** Work done in one day, one hour, or one minute is the efficiency for that unit of time.

---

## 3. Three Most Important Formulas

The main formula is:

Work = Efficiency × Time

From this, we get:

1. Total Work = Efficiency × Time
2. Efficiency = Total Work / Time
3. Time = Total Work / Efficiency

These three formulas are the foundation of the chapter.

---

## 4. The LCM Method

When the time taken by different people to complete the same work is given, we can assume:

**Total Work = LCM of their individual completion times**

This makes calculations easier when the times are whole numbers.

### Example

A can complete a job in 10 days.
B can complete the same job in 20 days.

Step 1: Find the LCM.

LCM of 10 and 20 = 20

Assume Total Work = 20 units.

Step 2: Find individual efficiencies.

A's Efficiency = 20 / 10 = 2 units per day

B's Efficiency = 20 / 20 = 1 unit per day

Step 3: Add their efficiencies.

Combined Efficiency = 2 + 1 = 3 units per day

Step 4: Find the time taken together.

Time = Total Work / Combined Efficiency

Time = 20 / 3 days

**Answer = 6⅔ days**

### Important Note

Choose a convenient total work that is divisible by the individual completion times. The LCM is usually the easiest choice.

---

## 5. When Multiple People Work Together

When people work together, add their efficiencies.

### Formula

Combined Efficiency = A's Efficiency + B's Efficiency

Time Taken = Total Work / Combined Efficiency

### Example

A completes a job in 12 days.
B completes the same job in 6 days.

Assume Total Work = LCM(12, 6) = 12 units.

A's Efficiency = 12 / 12 = 1 unit per day

B's Efficiency = 12 / 6 = 2 units per day

Combined Efficiency = 1 + 2 = 3 units per day

Time = 12 / 3 = 4 days

**Answer: 4 days**

### Three People Working Together

If A, B, and C work together:

Combined Efficiency = A + B + C

Time = Total Work / (A + B + C)

Always use their efficiencies, not their completion times, when adding their contributions.

---

## 6. Relationship Between Time and Efficiency

For the same total work:

**Time and Efficiency are inversely proportional.**

This means:
- Higher efficiency → Less time
- Lower efficiency → More time

### Important Formula

If the ratio of A's time to B's time is:

A's Time : B's Time = a : b

Then:

A's Efficiency : B's Efficiency = b : a

The ratio is reversed.

### Example

A completes work in 10 days.
B completes the same work in 20 days.

Time ratio = 10 : 20 = 1 : 2

Efficiency ratio = 2 : 1

A is twice as efficient as B.

---

## 7. Work Done in a Given Number of Days

If the efficiency of a person is known:

Work Done = Efficiency × Number of Days

### Example

A's efficiency is 4 units per day.

How much work will A complete in 5 days?

Work Done = 4 × 5 = 20 units

If the total work is 60 units:

Remaining Work = 60 − 20 = 40 units

---

## 8. When Someone Leaves After Working for Some Days

In these questions:
1. Find the total work.
2. Find each person's efficiency.
3. Calculate the work completed before the person leaves.
4. Subtract it from the total work.
5. Divide the remaining work by the efficiency of the person who continues.

### Example

A can complete a job in 16 days.
B can complete it in 12 days.

They work together for 4 days. Then A leaves.

How many more days will B need?

Step 1: Total Work

LCM(16, 12) = 48 units

Step 2: Efficiencies

A = 48 / 16 = 3 units per day

B = 48 / 12 = 4 units per day

Step 3: Combined Efficiency

3 + 4 = 7 units per day

Step 4: Work completed in 4 days

7 × 4 = 28 units

Step 5: Remaining Work

48 − 28 = 20 units

Step 6: Time taken by B

20 / 4 = 5 days

**Answer: B needs 5 more days.**

Total elapsed time = 4 + 5 = 9 days.

**Important:** Check whether the question asks for the remaining time or the total time.

---

## 9. When Someone Leaves Before the Work Is Completed

The same remaining-work method applies.

### Steps

1. Find the total work.
2. Calculate the combined efficiency.
3. Calculate the work completed before the person leaves.
4. Subtract the completed work from the total.
5. Divide the remaining work by the efficiency of the person who stays.

### Example

Total work = 48 units.

A's efficiency = 3 units per day.
B's efficiency = 4 units per day.

They work together for 4 days. A then leaves.

Work completed = (3 + 4) × 4 = 28 units

Remaining work = 48 − 28 = 20 units

B's remaining time = 20 / 4 = 5 days

Total time = 4 + 5 = 9 days.

---

## 10. When Someone Joins Later

When a new person joins:
1. Calculate the work completed before they join.
2. Subtract it from the total work.
3. Add the efficiencies of everyone working after they join.
4. Calculate the remaining time.

### Formula

Remaining Time = Remaining Work / New Combined Efficiency

If A works alone initially and B joins later:

- Initial efficiency = A's efficiency
- New efficiency = A's efficiency + B's efficiency

Be careful about when the second person starts working.

---

## 11. Working Lines

A working line is a helpful way to track work when people join or leave.

Draw a timeline and mark:
- Total work
- Who works during each period
- Duration of each period
- Work completed during each period
- Remaining work

### Example Structure

Total Work = 100 units

Period 1:
A and B work together for 4 days.

Work completed = (A's efficiency + B's efficiency) × 4

Period 2:
A leaves, and B finishes the remaining work.

Remaining Work = Total Work − Work completed in Period 1

Time in Period 2 = Remaining Work / B's efficiency

Total Time = Period 1 + Period 2

**Tip:** Draw a working line whenever the question contains words such as:
- After
- Before
- Leaves
- Joins
- Remaining work
- Together for some days

---

## 12. Fraction of Work

Sometimes a question states that a person or group completes a fraction of the work in a certain time.

### Formula

If a person completes `a/b` of the work in `T` days:

Time for the whole work = T × b/a

### Example

A completes 2/5 of a job in 4 days.

Time for the complete job:

= 4 × 5/2

= 10 days

### Another Example

A and B together complete 8/15 of a job in 5 days.

Time for the whole job:

= 5 × 15/8

= 75/8 days

= 9⅜ days

If the question asks how long B alone takes, you need additional information about B's efficiency or the remaining-work arrangement.

**Important:** Read whether the question asks for:
- A fraction of the work
- The remaining work
- The complete work

Do not confuse these three.

---

## 13. Efficiency Ratios

Efficiency ratios are useful when the question says that one person is twice or three times as efficient as another.

### Example

A is twice as efficient as B.

If B's efficiency is 1 unit per day:

A's efficiency = 2 units per day

Efficiency ratio:

A : B = 2 : 1

### Another Example

A is three times as efficient as B.

Efficiency ratio:

A : B = 3 : 1

If the ratio is given, use convenient numbers that satisfy the ratio.

For example, if A : B = 4 : 3, assume:
- A's efficiency = 4 units per day
- B's efficiency = 3 units per day

These are relative values; they do not necessarily represent actual units until the total work or another condition is known.

---

## 14. Men, Women, and Children

Some questions compare the work done by different categories of workers.

The main principle remains the same:

**Total Work = Total Efficiency × Time**

If the efficiency of one man, one woman, and one child is known, calculate the combined efficiency by adding the contributions of the required workers.

### Example Structure

Suppose:
- One man has efficiency M.
- One woman has efficiency W.
- One child has efficiency C.

Then:

Efficiency of 2 men and 3 women = 2M + 3W

Efficiency of 1 man, 2 women, and 1 child:

= M + 2W + C

If total work is known:

Time = Total Work / Combined Efficiency

**Important:** Do not assume that a man, woman, or child has a particular efficiency unless the question provides the relationship.

---

## 15. Men, Days, and Hours

For questions involving the number of workers, days, and hours, use the idea that total work depends on the total worker-time contribution, assuming the workers have equal efficiency and work at the same rate.

### Basic Relationship

Work ∝ Number of Workers × Days × Hours per Day

For the same work:

M₁ × D₁ × H₁ = M₂ × D₂ × H₂

Where:
- M = Number of workers
- D = Number of days
- H = Working hours per day

### Example

10 workers complete a job in 6 days, working 8 hours per day.

How many days will 20 equally efficient workers need if they work 6 hours per day?

Use:

10 × 6 × 8 = 20 × D × 6

480 = 120D

D = 4 days

**Answer: 4 days**

This relationship assumes the same kind of work and equal individual efficiency.

---

## 16. Important Shortcuts

### Shortcut 1: Same Work

For the same work:

Time Ratio = Reverse of Efficiency Ratio

### Shortcut 2: Work Together

Add efficiencies first.

Then:

Time = Total Work / Combined Efficiency

### Shortcut 3: Someone Leaves

Remaining Work = Total Work − Work Already Completed

Remaining Time = Remaining Work / Efficiency of the people who stay

### Shortcut 4: Someone Joins

New Combined Efficiency = Old Efficiency + New Person's Efficiency

Use it only from the time the new person starts working.

### Shortcut 5: Fraction of Work

If `a/b` of the work takes T days:

Whole-work time = T × b/a

### Shortcut 6: LCM

When individual completion times are given, the LCM is usually a convenient choice for total work.

### Shortcut 7: Check the Wording

- "How many more days?" → Remaining time
- "How many days in total?" → Include the earlier days
- "After 4 days" → The first period lasts 4 days
- "Before completion" → Track the remaining work carefully

---

## 17. Common Mistakes

1. Adding completion times instead of efficiencies.
2. Forgetting to reverse the time ratio to obtain the efficiency ratio.
3. Forgetting to subtract completed work from total work.
4. Using the combined efficiency after a person has left.
5. Adding the initial days when the question asks only for remaining days.
6. Forgetting to include initial days when the question asks for total elapsed time.
7. Treating a fraction of work as if it were the whole work.
8. Using the wrong person's efficiency for the remaining work.
9. Mixing hours and days without converting the units.
10. Assuming workers have equal efficiency when the question does not say so.

---

## 1. Core Formulas (Mother formula)

Maan lo ek aadmi 1 din me 20 khilone banata hai. 10 din me → 20 × 10 = **200 khilone**.

| Term | Matlab | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Efficiency (E)** | 1 din me kiya gaya kaam | 20 khilone/day |

| **Total Work (W)** | Kul kaam | 200 khilone |

### Teen formulas (ek hi cheez ke 3 roop)

- **Work = Efficiency × Time**
- **Efficiency = Work / Time**
- **Time = Work / Efficiency**

> **Note:** Total Work hamesha **numerator (upar)** me hota hai.
> Formula **rata nahi, banaya hai**: E × T = W → baaki do isi se nikalte hain.

## 2. Efficiency kya hoti hai?

- Hindi: **Karyakshamta / Dakshta**
- Definition: **Work done per unit time** (samay ki ek ikai me kiya gaya kaam)
- Unit time = 1 second, 1 minute, 1 hour, 1 day, 1 month, 1 year (time ke aage "1" lagao to wo efficiency ban jati hai).

**Real life me:** Company salary efficiency par deti hai. Ek ghante me 4 rotiyan khaate ho to tumhari efficiency = 4 rotiyan/hour.

---

## 3. Time aur Efficiency – Inverse Relation

- Jo **kam time** leta hai uski **efficiency zyada** hoti hai.
- Jo **zyada time** leta hai uski **efficiency kam** hoti hai.

**Formula:** Time ∝ 1/Efficiency

**Ratio trick:** Agar Time ratio **A : B** hai to Efficiency ratio **B : A** (ulta, "palat do").

**Example:** A 10 din, B 20 din ⇒ Time ratio 10 : 20 = 1 : 2 ⇒ Efficiency ratio = **2 : 1**.

---

## 4. Kyun LCM Nikalte Hain? (Real Reason)

**Problem:** A ek room 10 din me saaf karta hai, B 20 din me. Dono mil jaye to?
Galat soch: 10 + 20 = 30 din ❌.
Sahi soch: Dono mil kar kaam kar rahe hain to time **kam** lagega (**30 se kam**).

**Reason:** **Time ulti disha me chalta hai** (inverse), isliye time ko add nahi kar sakte.
**Efficiency seedhi disha me chalti hai**, isliye efficiency ko add kar sakte hain (₹ ki tarah).

**Isiliye:**
1. Time ka **LCM** nikalo → ye hamara **Total Work** ban jata hai.
2. **Efficiency** nikalo (Work ÷ time).
3. Ab sab kuch **efficiency aur work** ke saath karo (time ke saath nahi).

> **Time se bachke efficiency par aao, tabhi fansne se bachoge.**

---

## 5. Standard Method (Steps)

**Step 1:** Individual times ka **LCM** = Total Work
**Step 2:** Har ek ki **Efficiency = Work / Time**
**Step 3:** Jo bhi puchha ho uske hisaab se efficiency add/subtract karo
**Step 4:** **Time = Total Work / Combined Efficiency**

### Ex1: A = 10 days, B = 20 days. Together?
- LCM(10, 20) = **20** (Total Work)
- Eff A = 20/10 = **2**, Eff B = 20/20 = **1**
- Together eff = 3
- Time = 20/3 = **6.67 days** (6⅔)

### Ex2: A = 10, B = 20, C = 15 days. All together?
- LCM(10, 20, 15) = **60**
- Eff A = 6, B = 3, C = 4 → Total = **13**
- Time = **60/13 days**

### Ex3: A = 12, B = 15, C = 20 days. All together?
- LCM = 60
- Eff = 5, 4, 3 → Total = **12**
- Time = 60/12 = **5 days**

---

## 6. Shortcut: Sirf 2 logon (A aur B) ka time diya ho

**Formula:** **Time (A + B) = (A × B) / (A + B)**

**Ex:** A = 12, B = 15
- Time = (12 × 15)/(12 + 15) = 180/27 = **20/3 days** (same answer ✔)

> **Warning:** Ye shortcut sirf **2 logon (A + B)** ke liye hai. **3 logon (A + B + C) par kaam nahi karta.**

---

## 7. Part of Work Wale Questions

**Rule:** Agar x% / fraction kaam d din me hota hai, to **poora kaam** = d ÷ fraction.

### Q: A, B, C milkar kaam ka 66⅔% (=2/3) 2 din me, A+B milkar 30% (=3/10) 3 din me karte hain. C akela total work ka **4⅔ guna** kaam kitne din me karega?

**Step 1 (Time nikalo):**
- ABC: 2/3 kaam 2 din me → poora kaam **3 din**
- AB: 3/10 kaam 3 din me → poora kaam **10 din**

**Step 2:** LCM(3, 10) = **30** (Total Work)
- Eff (ABC) = 30/3 = **10**
- Eff (AB) = 30/10 = **3**
- Eff C = 10 − 3 = **7**

**Step 3:** C ko karna hai **4⅔ × 30 = 14/3 × 30 = 140** units
- Time = 140/7 = **20 days**

**Trap:** Yahan "4⅔" ka matlab **percentage nahi**, "4⅔ guna total work" hai (zyada kaam). C ne "bachcha" samajh ke hal kar diya, par kaam 30 nahi 140 hai!

## 8. Pair-wise Time (A+B, B+C, C+A)

### Q: A+B = 3 days, B+C = 4 days, C+A = 6 days. A+B+C together? A alone? B alone? C alone?

- LCM(3, 4, 6) = **12** (Total Work)
- Eff (A+B) = 4, (B+C) = 3, (C+A) = 2
- Add all three = 2(A + B + C) = 4 + 3 + 2 = 9
- **Eff (A+B+C) = 9/2 = 4.5**
- **Time (A+B+C) = 12 / 4.5 = 8/3 days**

**Alone nikalne ka trick (efficiency subtract):**
- Eff A = (A+B+C) − (B+C) = 4.5 − 3 = **1.5** → Time A = 12/1.5 = **8 days**
- Eff B = 4.5 − (C+A) = 4.5 − 2 = **2.5** → Time B = 12/2.5 = **4.8 days**
- Eff C = 4.5 − (A+B) = 4.5 − 4 = **0.5** → Time C = 12/0.5 = **24 days**

> **Trick:** Jis ki efficiency chahiye, usko "chhod ke" baaki do ki pair-wise efficiency (A+B+C se) subtract karo.

## 9. Leaving / Joining – Working Line

**Use karo jab:** koi beech me chhod de ya beech me aaye.

### Ex1 (After case): Aman = 16 days, Babita = 12 days. Dono ne 4 din saath kaam kiya, phir Aman chala gaya. Babita ne total kitne din kaam kiya?

- LCM(16, 12) = **48** (Total Work)
- Eff Aman = 3, Babita = 4
- Saath kaam: 3 + 4 = 7/day × 4 days = **28 units**
- Bacha = 48 − 28 = **20 units**
- Babita akeli: 20/4 = **5 days**
- **Babita ka total time = 4 + 5 = 9 days**

**Alternate (bina line):**
- Aman ne 4 din × 3 = 12 units kiye. Bacha = 48 − 12 = 36.
- Babita **shuru se aakhir tak** kaam karti rahi → 36/4 = **9 days** ✔

### Ex2 (Before case): Aman = 8 days, Babita = 12 days. Dono ne saath shuru kiya, par **Aman completion se 2 din pehle** chhod gaya. Total time?

- LCM(8, 12) = **24** (Total Work)
- Eff Aman = 3, Babita = 2
- Aakhir ke 2 din Babita akeli: 2 × 2 = **4 units**
- Bacha = 24 − 4 = **20 units** → dono ne milke
- Combined eff = 5 → 20/5 = **4 days**
- **Total = 4 + 2 = 6 days**

## 10. After vs Before – Special Trick

**Rule:**
- **"After" case** (kaam karne ke baad chhod gaya) → uska kiya hua kaam **total work me se minus** kar do.
- **"Before" case** (kaam khatam hone se pehle chhod gaya) → usko **bitha lo, uska extra kaam total work me plus** kar do. (Chhodne wale ko jaane hi mat do.)

### Ex2 ko shortcut se (Before case):
- Aman ko bitha liya: aakhri 2 din ka kaam = 3 × 2 = **6 units extra**
- Total work = 24 + 6 = **30**
- Ab dono ne pura time saath kaam kiya (eff = 5) → 30/5 = **6 days** ✔

### Triple case (Before + After dono):
**Q:** A = 10, B = 12, C = 15 days. Teeno ne saath shuru kiya. 3 din baad C ne chhod diya. Kaam khatam hone se 4 din pehle B ne chhod diya. Total time?

- LCM = **60**; Eff A = 6, B = 5, C = 4
- **C → After case** (3 din baad gaya): total me se minus → C ne sirf 3 din kaam kiya = 4 × 3 = **12 units**
- **B → Before case** (4 din pehle gaya): B ko bitha lo → total me plus 5 × 4 = **20 units extra**
- Total work effectively = 60 − 12 + 20 = **68**
- Ab C jaa chuka hai, B ko humne bitha liya, isliye **A + B ne** poora kaam kiya (eff = 6 + 5 = 11)
- Time = **68/11 days** (video me verified answer)

> **Note:** Is type me **working line fail** ho sakti hai (extra work aa jata hai). Isliye After/Before ka add-subtract trick use karo.

**Equation form (agar samajhna ho):** Agar total time T hai: A ne T din kaam kiya (6T), B ne (T − 4) din (5(T − 4)), C ne sirf 3 din (12). Total: 6T + 5T − 20 + 12 = 60 → **11T = 68** → **T = 68/11** ✔

## 11. Alternate Days

### Q: Aman = 10 days, Babita = 20 days. Aman shuru karta hai, phir baari-baari (alternate days) kaam karte hain. Total kitne din?

- LCM = 20; Eff Aman = 2, Babita = 1
- 2 din (A, B) ka kaam = 2 + 1 = **3 units**
- Chahiye 20 units. 6 chakar (12 days) = 6 × 3 = **18 units**
- Bacha = 2 units. Agla din (13th day) Aman kaam karega, uski eff = 2 → **2 units done**
- **Total = 13 days**

> 6 se multiply kyu? Kyunki 20 ke sabse **close** (par 20 se aage nahi) pahunchna hai. 7 lete to 21 ho jata (aage nikal jata).

## 12. "Twice as good as" Type

### Q: Aman, Babita se **do guna achha** hai. Dono milke 50 din me kaam karte hain. Aman akela kitne din?

- "Twice as good" = **efficiency 2 : 1** (Aman : Babita)
- Combined eff = 3
- Total work = 3 × 50 = **150**
- Aman alone = 150/2 = **75 days**

## 13. MDH Formula (Men – Days – Hours)

### Formula:

**(M₁ × D₁ × H₁) / W₁ = (M₂ × D₂ × H₂) / W₂**

- **M** = log (men), **D** = days, **H** = hours/day, **W** = work
- Sirf jitne variables question me hain, utne hi use karo (jaise hours na ho to H hata do).
- **W hamesha neeche rahega.**

### Q1: 70 men kaam 6 days me karte hain. 7 men **aadha kaam** kitne din me?
- W₁ = 2 (poora), W₂ = 1 (aadha) (relative)
- (70 × 6)/2 = (7 × D₂)/1
- 210 = 7D₂ → **D₂ = 30 days**

### Q2: 70 men, 8 hr/day, 9 din me kaam. 5 din baad pata chala sirf **40%** kaam hua. Baaki kaam 4 din me. Kitne **ghante/din** kaam karna padega?
- Pehla part: 70 × 8 × 5 / 40 (W₁ = 40%)
- Dusra part: 70 × H₂ × 4 / 60 (W₂ = 60%, baaki)
- **Note:** 9 days total hai, par pehle **5 days** use hue, baaki **4 days** bache. Yahan D₁ = 5, D₂ = 4 (9 nahi lena!)
- (70 × 8 × 5)/40 = (70 × H₂ × 4)/60
- 70 aur 70 cut → (8 × 5)/40 = (4 × H₂)/60
- 1 = (4H₂)/60 → **H₂ = 15 hours/day**

### Q3: Alag-alag persons (jaise men aur women)
**Q:** 30 women, 9 hr/day, 10 din me 1000 parcels pack karti hain. Women ki efficiency men se **100/3 % (=1/3) zyada**. 1500 parcels, 5 hr/day, 10 din me → kitne men?
- Men eff = 3, Women eff = 4 (kyunki women = men + 1/3 of men)
- Equation: (30 × 4 × 9 × 10)/1000 = (x × 3 × 5 × 10)/1500
- 10.8 = x/10 → **x = 108 men**

*(Yahan **dono ki alag efficiency** hai isliye M ki jagah efficiency × log likha. Same type ke log hote to seedha number likhte.)*

## 14. Group Equivalence – "OR" and "AND" Cases

### Q: 6 men **ya** 2 women **ya** 4 boys kaam 77 din me kar sakte hain. 1 man + 2 women + 1 boy (saath) kitne din me?

**Method 1 (Efficiency via Hide & Seek):**
- 6 men = 2 women = 4 boys ⇒ ratios of efficiency: (Hide & Seek / ulta LCM)
- LCM(6, 2, 4) = 12
- Man : Woman : Boy = 12/6 : 12/2 : 12/4 = **2 : 6 : 3**
- Total work = 6 men × 2 eff × 77 days = **924**
- Combined = 1 man + 2 women + 1 boy = 2 + (2×6) + 3 = **17**
- Time = 924/17 days

**Method 2 (OR–AND shortcut):**
- **"OR"** ke liye time wahi diya hai (77).
- **"AND"** ke liye: 77 ÷ (1/6 + 2/2 + 1/4) (jo "and" me hain unki sankhya / jo "or" me hain unki sankhya)
- = 77 ÷ [(2 + 12 + 3)/12] = 77 × 12/17 = **924/17 days** ✔

**Rule:** "Or" wale group ki sankhya (6, 2, 4) neeche likho. "And" wale group ki sankhya (1, 2, 1) upar. Sab ko +. Phir given days ko us total se divide karo.

## 15. x Wale Questions (Time x + something)

### Q: A aur B milkar x din me. A akela (x + 16) din me. B akela (x + 9) din me. x = ?

**Formula (shortcut):**

**x = √(16 × 9) = √144 = 12**

**Rule:** Agar A akela (x + a) aur B akela (x + b) hai, to **x = √(a × b)**.

**Check (basic se):** 1/x = 1/(x+16) + 1/(x+9) → quadratic → x² = 144 → x = 12 ✔

## 16. Wages / Salary Distribution

**Rule:** Salary ka distribution **do cheezon** par hota hai:
1. **Efficiency**
2. **Kitne din kaam kiya**

**Salary ratio = (Efficiency ratio) × (Time ratio)**

### Q: A aur B ne 10 din milke kaam kiya, total ₹27,000 mile. Efficiency ratio 5 : 4. A ki per day salary?
- Dono ne **10 din** kaam kiya → time same → ignore.
- Salary split ratio = **5 : 4**
- A ka share = 27,000 × 5/9 = **₹15,000** (10 din ka)
- **Per day** = 15,000/10 = **₹1,500**

(Video me ₹2700 per day total nikala: 27,000/10 = 2700; A ka share 5/9 × 2700 = **₹1500**)

## 17. Word Problems (Pages, Parcels etc.)

### Q1: A aur B milkar 88 pages 11 ghante me padhte hain. B ki efficiency A se **40% kam**. A+B+C milkar 150 pages 10 ghante me padhte hain. B + C milkar 120 pages kitne ghante me?
- Eff (A + B) = 88/11 = **8**
- B = A − 40% of A → A : B = 5 : 3 (A = 5, B = 3)
- Eff (A + B + C) = 150/10 = **15**
- Eff C = 15 − 8 = **7**
- Eff (B + C) = 3 + 7 = **10**
- Time = 120/10 = **12 hours**

### Q2: A akela kaam 40 din me. A + B milke kaam ka 1/4 hissa 3 din me. A ne akele shuru kiya, 8 din baad B juda. Baaki kaam A+B kitne din me?
- A + B: 1/4 kaam 3 din me → poora **12 din**
- LCM(40, 12) = **120** (Total Work)
- Eff A = 3, Eff (A + B) = 10
- A ne 8 din akele: 8 × 3 = **24 units**
- Bacha = 120 − 24 = **96 units**
- Dono milke: 96/10 = **9.6 days**

### Q3: A+B ek kaam ka 8/15 hissa 5 din me kar lete hain. Baaki kaam B akela 7 din me kar leta hai. A akela poora kaam kitne din me?
- Total kaam = **75** (numerators ka LCM: 75 and 15 → 75)
- (A+B) 8/15 kaam 5 din me → poora kaam 5 × 15/8 = **75/8 din** → Eff (A + B) = 75 ÷ (75/8) = **8**
- Baaki 7/15 kaam B ne 7 din me kiya → B poora kaam 15 din me → Eff B = 75/15 = **5**
- Eff A = 8 − 5 = **3**
- **A alone = 75/3 = 25 days**

**Fraction wale kaam me:** Total work ke liye **numerator ka LCM** lo (jaise 75).

### Q4: P akela 40 din. Efficiency ratio P : Q = 4 : 5. Q ne kaam shuru kiya, 12 din baad chhod diya. Baaki kaam R ne 30 din me kiya. R akela kitne din?
- P ka total work = 4 × 40 = **160** units
- Q ne 12 din × 5 = **60 units** kiye
- Baaki = **100 units** R ne 30 din me → Eff R = 100/30 = **10/3**
- R alone = 160 ÷ (10/3) = 160 × 3/10 = **48 days**

## 18. Mains Level Question

### Q: Kuch men (x) ek kaam boys (y) se **2 din pehle** khatam karte hain. 1 man = 3 boys (efficiency). Konsa ratio men : boys is condition ko satisfy karega: 1 : 2, 2 : 5, 2 : 3, 16 : 35?

**Setup:**
- Maan lo boys d din lete hain, to men (d − 2) din lete hain.
- Total work same → **men × eff × time = boys × eff × time**
- x × 3 × (d − 2) = y × 1 × d
- **d (3x − y) = 6x** → **d = 6x/(3x − y)**

**Condition:** d **positive** hona chahiye.

| x : y | d = 6x/(3x − y) | Valid? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 : 2 | 6/(3 − 2) = 6 | ✔ |
| 2 : 5 | 12/(6 − 5) = 12 | ✔ |
| 2 : 3 | 12/(6 − 3) = 4 | ✔ |
| 16 : 35 | 96/(48 − 35) = 96/13 | ✔ |

**Answer: All options satisfy.** (d fraction me ho sakta hai.)

## 20. Common Mistakes

1. **Time ko add kar dena** (10 + 20 = 30). Time ulta chalta hai, efficiency add karo.
2. **LCM bhoolna ya galat lena** — total work ke liye hamesha LCM.
3. **A+B+C ke case me AB/(A+B) shortcut lagana** — ye sirf 2 logon ke liye hai.
4. **"4⅔ of total work" ko 4⅔% samajhna** — wo kaam 4⅔ guna hai.
5. **MDH me poore 9 days lena** jabki 5 din pehle ho chuke ho — D₁ = 5, D₂ = 4.
6. **Efficiency alag-alag hone par bhi seedhe numbers likh dena** (Q3 wale case me M ke saath uski efficiency bhi multiply karo).
7. **"Before" case me working line lagana** — wahan bitha lo aur extra work add karo.
8. **Wages me sirf efficiency ya sirf time dekhna** — dono dekho.
9. **Question padhne me jaldbazi** — "alone" ya "together" aur "hours/day" dhyan se.

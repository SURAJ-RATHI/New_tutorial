# 1. What is Direction & Distance?

In this chapter, questions mainly ask us to find:

1. **Direction**
2. **Distance**

For example:

- In which direction is a person moving?
- Which direction is a person facing?
- How far is a person from the starting point?
- In which direction is one point with respect to another point?
- What is the shortest distance between two points?

The chapter contains **9 major types of questions**.

The first 4 types are the most important according to the lecture.

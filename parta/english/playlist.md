# 1. SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT

## 1.1 Basic Concept

Subject = जो काम करता है / जिसके बारे में बात हो रही है.

Verb = जो action या state बताता है.

Example:

He plays cricket.

Subject = He
Verb = plays

---

# 1.2 The Most Important Rule

### Singular Subject → Singular Verb

He plays.
She works.
Ram goes.

### Plural Subject → Plural Verb

They play.
We work.
The boys go.

### 🔥 Exam Trick

Present Simple में:

HE / SHE / IT
        ↓
verb में S/ES

I / WE / YOU / THEY
        ↓
verb में S/ES नहीं

Example:

He go ❌
He goes ✅

They goes ❌
They go ✅

---

# 1.3 सबसे बड़ा Exam Trap

Sentence:

The quality of the apples ___ good.

Options:

A. are
B. is
C. were
D. have

पहली नजर में "apples" देखकर बहुत लोग ARE चुनेंगे.

लेकिन actual subject है:

The quality

"of the apples" सिर्फ एक prepositional phrase है.

Therefore:

The quality of the apples IS good. ✅

### 🔥 Rule

> Subject के बीच में आने वाले `of + noun` को हटाकर
> असली subject पहचानो.

Examples:

The quality of the products **is** good.

The list of candidates **is** ready.

The price of these books **is** high.

---

# 1.4 EACH / EVERY

Each + singular noun

Every + singular noun

Therefore:

Each student **is** present. ✅

Every boy **has** a book. ✅

NOT:

Each student are ❌

Every boy have ❌

### 🔥 Memory Trick

> EACH / EVERY देखते ही
> अगला noun और verb दोनों पर ध्यान दो.

---

# 1.5 EITHER / NEITHER

Either of + plural noun
→ verb generally singular in standard exam grammar.

Neither of the boys **is** ready.

Either of the answers **is** correct.

### Exam Trap

"boys" plural देखकर ARE मत लगाना.

Main structure:

Neither + of + plural noun + singular verb

---

# 1.6 A NUMBER OF vs THE NUMBER OF

### A number of

Meaning = many

Therefore plural verb:

A number of students **are** absent.

### The number of

Meaning = संख्या

Therefore singular verb:

The number of students **is** increasing.

### 🔥 Super Trick

A number of → MANY → ARE

The number of → COUNT → IS

---

# 1.7 Collective Noun

Words like:

team
committee
family
government
jury
class

Context देखना जरूरी है.

If group is treated as one unit:

The team **is** winning.

If individual members are emphasised, context may affect agreement in some varieties.

### Exam Strategy

SSC-style questions में sentence structure और intended sense को देखकर answer करो.
Blindly "collective noun = singular" मत रटो.

---

# 1.8 SUBJECT को पहचानने की 5-SECOND METHOD

Sentence देखकर:

### Step 1
Main verb खोजो.

### Step 2
Verb से पूछो:

"Who/what is doing this?"

### Step 3
उस noun/pronoun को subject मानो.

### Step 4
बीच के phrases ignore करो.

### Step 5
Subject singular/plural check करो.

---

# 1.9 Practice

### Q1
The list of candidates ___ ready.

A. are
B. is
C. were
D. have

Answer: B

Reason:

Subject = list
List = singular

Therefore:

The list of candidates **is** ready.

---

### Q2
A number of students ___ absent.

A. is
B. was
C. are
D. has

Answer: C

Reason:

A number of = many

Therefore plural verb.

# 2. TENSE

## 2.1 Tense क्या बताता है?

Tense हमें बताता है:

> Action कब हुआ?

Three basic time zones:

PAST → PRESENT → FUTURE

---

# 2.2 Present Simple

### Structure

Subject + V1/V1+s/es

I play.
They play.
He plays.

### Uses

1. Habit
2. Universal truth
3. General fact
4. Routine

Examples:

I go to school every day.

The sun rises in the east.

Water boils at 100°C.

### 🔥 Trigger Words

often
usually
always
every day
generally
sometimes

---

# 2.3 Present Continuous

### Structure

Subject + is/am/are + V-ing

I am reading.

He is playing.

They are working.

### Use

Action happening around now.

Example:

She is studying now.

### 🔥 Trigger

now
at present
currently
at the moment

---

# 2.4 Present Perfect

### Structure

Subject + has/have + V3

I have finished the work.

She has completed the task.

### Important

Present Perfect usually connects a completed action with the present.

### Common confusion

"I have seen him yesterday." ❌

"Yesterday" is a finished past time, so Simple Past is normally required:

I saw him yesterday. ✅

---

# 2.5 Past Simple

### Structure

Subject + V2

I went.

She worked.

They played.

### Trigger

yesterday
last week
ago
in 2010
previously

---

# 2.6 Future Simple

Subject + will + V1

I will go.

She will come.

They will study.

---

# 2.7 🔥 TENSE EXAM METHOD

Don't blindly search for the tense formula.

First find:

1. Time expression
2. Main action
3. Whether action is complete
4. Whether action is continuing
5. Whether it is habitual

Then choose the tense.

# 3. NOUN

## 3.1 Noun क्या है?

A noun names:

- person
- place
- thing
- animal
- idea
- quality
- feeling

Examples:

Ram
Delhi
book
dog
honesty
beauty

---

# 3.2 Countable vs Uncountable

### Countable

Can be counted individually.

one book
two books
three books

### Uncountable

Not normally counted individually.

information
advice
furniture
equipment
knowledge

### 🔥 Exam Trap

"an advice" ❌

"an information" ❌

Correct:

a piece of advice

a piece of information

# 4. PRONOUN

## Pronoun = noun की जगह आने वाला word

Ram is a student.

Ram is intelligent.

Better:

Ram is a student.

**He** is intelligent.

He = pronoun.

---

# 4.1 Important Pronoun Rule

Pronoun must agree with its antecedent in:

- number
- person
- gender where relevant

Example:

Every student must bring **his/her** book.

Exam questions may test pronoun reference and agreement.

---

# 6. ADVERB

Adverb can modify:

- verb

Example:

### 🔥 Trick

Adverb को केवल "-ly" देखकर पहचानना गलत है.

Some adverbs do not end in -ly:

fast

# 8. ARTICLES

## A / AN

Indefinite article.

Used mainly before singular countable nouns when not referring to a particular identified item.

a book

a university

an apple

an hour

### 🔥 Sound Rule

Article spelling से नहीं, **sound** से decide करो.

`an hour` ✅

because "hour" begins with vowel sound.

`a university` ✅

because "university" begins with /yuː/ sound.

---

# 8.1 THE

Use when the noun is specific/identifiable from context.

Example:

I saw a dog.

The dog was black.

First mention → a dog

Now identified → the dog

---

# 9. ACTIVE / PASSIVE VOICE

## Active

Subject performs the action.

Ram wrote a letter.

Ram = doer
letter = receiver

## Passive

Receiver becomes the focus.

A letter was written by Ram.

---

# 9.1 BASIC FORMULA

Active:

Subject + Verb + Object

Passive:

Object + helping verb + V3 + by + Subject

Example:

Ram writes a letter.

↓

A letter is written by Ram.

---

# 9.2 🔥 GOLDEN RULE

Passive voice में main verb की **V3** form आती है.

write → written

eat → eaten

do → done

make → made

---

# 9.3 Tense Conversion

Present Simple:

Active:
He writes a letter.

Passive:
A letter is written by him.

Past Simple:

He wrote a letter.

A letter was written by him.

Future Simple:

He will write a letter.

A letter will be written by him.

---

# 9.4 MODAL

Active:

He can do the work.

Passive:

The work can be done by him.

### Formula

Modal + V1

↓

Modal + be + V3

---

# 10. DIRECT / INDIRECT SPEECH

## Direct

He said, "I am busy."

Exact words are quoted.

## Indirect

He said that he was busy.

The exact quotation is removed and grammar changes according to the reporting context.

---

# 10.1 BASIC CHANGES

Present → Past

is → was

are → were

am → was

has/have → had

V1 → V2 in relevant backshift contexts

will → would

can → could

may → might

---

# 10.2 PRONOUN CHANGE

Direct:

He said, "I am tired."

Indirect:

He said that he was tired.

"I" changes according to the speaker.

### 🔥 Golden Rule

> Pronoun को blindly मत बदलो.
> Speaker और listener की relationship देखो.

---

# 11. CONDITIONALS

## Zero Conditional

General truth.

If + Present, Present

If you heat ice, it melts.

---

## First Conditional

Real/possible future.

If + Present, will + V1

If you work hard, you will succeed.

### Trap

Don't normally use `will` immediately after the if-clause in standard first conditional construction.

---

## Second Conditional

Unreal/hypothetical present/future.

If + Past, would + V1

If I were rich, I would travel.

### 🔥 Exam Trick

Formal hypothetical:

If I **were** you...

not:

If I was you...

---

## Third Conditional

Unreal past.

If + had + V3, would have + V3

If I had studied, I would have passed.

Meaning:

I didn't study → I didn't pass.

# 12.1 CORRELATIVE CONJUNCTIONS

Common pairs:

### 🔥 Exam Rule

In many structures, maintain parallel grammatical construction.

Wrong:

He likes not only singing but also to dance.

Better:

He likes not only singing but also dancing.

# 13. PARAJUMBLE

Parajumble is not random guessing.

First identify:

1. Opening sentence
2. Mandatory pairs
3. Pronoun references
4. Connectors
5. Chronological order
6. Conclusion

---

# 13.1 Opening Sentence

Opening sentence usually introduces the topic.

It normally doesn't begin with:

However
Therefore
Thus
This
These
Such
He
They

because these often refer back to something.

### 🔥 But remember

This is a clue, not an absolute rule.

---

# 13.2 Mandatory Pair

If:

A introduces a concept

B says "this concept..."

Then:

A → B

is a strong pair.

---

# 13.3 Connector Method

Words like:

however
therefore
moreover
although
finally
thus

tell you the relationship between sentences.

### Example

He worked hard. **Therefore**, he succeeded.

"Therefore" cannot normally introduce the first idea because it expresses a conclusion.
